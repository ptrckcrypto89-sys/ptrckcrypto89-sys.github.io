Frequently asked questions

What you need to know.

Does the platform change my legal name for me?

No. NameChange Navigator provides independent educational planning guidance and organization. It does not file government applications, provide legal representation or replace an attorney.

What does the $19.95 workbook include?

A 58-page downloadable planning workbook with a motor-vehicle agency directory for all 50 states, federal references, printable checklists, worksheets and account-update templates. It is not a set of 50 independently verified legal procedures.

Does the workbook cover divorce or court-ordered name changes?

No. Divorce restoration, court-ordered changes, minors, adoption, birth-record corrections, gender-related records and other pathways are not included in this marriage-related workbook.

Do I have to share personal identity documents?

No. The public site does not request your Social Security number, driver license or legal records. The planned private checklist stores task progress in your browser.

Are government filing fees included?

No. State or federal agency fees are separate and set by the agencies involved. The workbook helps organize your research and tasks; it does not submit applications.

Is this a subscription or website membership?

No. It is a one-time $19.95 purchase of a downloadable PDF workbook. Checkout and PDF delivery are handled through Payhip.
