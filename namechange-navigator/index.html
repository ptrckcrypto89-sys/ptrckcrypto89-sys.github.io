Frequently asked questions

What you need to know.

Does the platform change my legal name for me?

No. NameChange Navigator provides independent educational planning guidance and organization. It does not file government applications, provide legal representation or replace an attorney.

Are all 50 marriage-related state guides available to purchase?

All 50 marriage-based guides are prepared. The paid website checkout and entitlement system are still being validated, so website purchases are not open yet.

Can I use the site for a divorce or court-ordered name change?

You can select those reasons to see their development status. Complete state-specific paid guides for them are not yet offered.

Do I have to share personal identity documents?

No. The public site does not request your Social Security number, driver license or legal records. The planned private checklist stores task progress in your browser.

Are government filing fees included in the planned $29?

No. State or federal agency charges are separate and are set by the agencies involved. The proposed payment is for proprietary organization and educational guidance only.

Is the planned $29 a subscription?

No subscription is planned. A single payment and ongoing nationwide access are the intended model, subject to successful checkout and delivery testing before launch.

Independent informational service. NameChange Navigator is not a government agency, does not prepare or file government applications, and is not a law firm.
