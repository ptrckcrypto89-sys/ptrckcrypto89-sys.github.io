Kentucky name change after marriage — verified government process

This guide concerns updating an adult’s legal name after a marriage supported by an official certificate. It does not substitute for a separate Kentucky district-court order when the requested name is outside what the marriage document proves. Always confirm documents with the processing agency before visiting.

1. Secure the official certified marriage record

For marriages recorded in Kentucky since June 1958, the Kentucky Cabinet for Health and Family Services (CHFS) Office of Vital Statistics provides certified marriage records. For older marriages, contact the county clerk where the marriage license was issued. The state requires names of both spouses, date and county of marriage, the requesting party’s mailing information, and the number of copies sought. The base search/certified copy fee is $6 per marriage certificate under 901 KAR 5:050; payment is a nonrefundable record-search fee even if the record is not found. Official online/phone ordering may include separate third-party processing costs; mail orders avoid unauthorized resellers. Order the certified government record, not a decorative certificate or ordinary photocopy. If married elsewhere, use that jurisdiction’s issuing agency.

2. Correct your Social Security record before Kentucky driver licensing

First apply to the Social Security Administration for an updated name. Eligible people can start online and may need an appointment or Form SS-5. Prepare original or issuing-agency-certified marriage evidence, proof of identity, and citizenship or lawful-status documentation if requested. The Kentucky Transportation Cabinet (KYTC) requires your current legal name to match SSA’s record before it will process the credential name change. The current KYTC page requests an updated Social Security card in addition to legal-name-change evidence; do not assume you may show only a submitted SSA application.

SSA name correction · SSA evidence requirements · KYTC SSA-first requirement

3. Appear in person at a Kentucky Driver Licensing Regional Office

Kentucky’s updated myDrive process does not allow legal-name changes online or by mail. Bring your updated Social Security card, government-certified marriage certificate or other recognized legal evidence, and your current driver credential; the agency may request the underlying chain of identity documents for REAL ID. A myDrive pre-application is optional. KYTC charges $15 for a driver-license name change and issues a temporary document while the permanent credential is mailed, usually within 10–15 business days. The 10-day rule on KYTC’s page is for address changes and should not be presented as a post-marriage name deadline.

Kentucky name-change requirements and $15 fee · REAL ID document guide

Kentucky ID cards have a different fee schedule. KYTC offers a replacement ID with a selectable term: 4-year standard ID $11.50, 4-year REAL ID $14, 8-year standard ID $23, or 8-year REAL ID $28. These ID prices are distinct from the $15 driver's-license name-change charge. Official Kentucky state-ID options

4. Check vehicle title and registration records through the county clerk

Kentucky processes motor-vehicle titles at the County Clerk’s office. KYTC states that when the name on your existing title differs from the name on your identification, you may establish that they refer to the same person with a marriage license or divorce decree (as an affidavit showing the connection). For correcting a misspelled name, KYTC requires the owner to present photo ID and the title to the county clerk of the owner’s residence. A newly adopted marital surname is not necessarily a misspelling; ask your county clerk whether and how to update the printed title, registration and lienholder information. Do not assume a mandatory duplicate title or a standard name-correction fee from rules addressing ownership transfers. Adding a spouse as an owner is a separate title transaction and may trigger tax/document requirements.

KYTC vehicle title rules and county clerk instructions

5. Update Kentucky voter registration before the election deadline

The State Board of Elections says voters must update their registration after a legal name change. Use the official online system at GoVoteKY, or send a completed new voter-registration application to the county clerk by mail or in person. Kentucky generally closes registration and updates 29 days before Election Day. For the November 3, 2026 general election, the official portal lists October 5, 2026 at 4:00 p.m. local time as the online deadline, which has already passed. Its official FAQ says the rolls are frozen after the registration deadline until after the next election; do not promise online changes before November 3. If you have a pending identity discrepancy, contact the county clerk about how to vote using the applicable rules.

6. Change your U.S. passport using the correct federal form

Review the U.S. Department of State’s DS-5504, DS-82, and DS-11 requirements. DS-5504 may be eligible when both the marriage-based legal-name change and passport issuance occurred within the previous year; otherwise the correct route depends on the passport’s condition, age, and issuance timing. You may need to submit a government-certified marriage document, most recent passport, photo and appropriate application fee. Verify travel timing before mailing a passport and match flight reservations to the document you will use.

7. Complete other identity and account changes

Notify your employer and payroll department, insurers, professional licensing agencies, financial institutions, health providers, schools, real-property offices and travel profiles separately. Ask each organization about required supporting evidence; government systems do not necessarily update these private accounts automatically. Avoid submitting sensitive documents to unofficial service sites.

When marriage documents are not enough: Kentucky adult name change

Under KRS 401.010, an adult generally petitions the district court in the county of residence for a legal name change outside the marriage certificate's established change. Kentucky Court of Justice guidance identifies form AOC-295, completed in duplicate and signed before a notary or probate clerk. Where the adult has valid photo ID, the court guide says an appearance normally is not required; where not, a hearing may be needed. Filing charges vary; contact the circuit court clerk for current fees before submitting the separate petition. Do not pay a court-petition fee just because you need a replacement credential reflecting a documented married name.

Kentucky KRS Chapter 401, governing court name changes · Kentucky Court of Justice adult name-change petition guide

Kentucky preparation checklist

  1. Order an official certified marriage certificate from CHFS or the recording jurisdiction.
  2. Update SSA and obtain your updated Social Security card.
  3. Bring marriage certificate, SSA card and ID to a KYTC Driver Licensing Regional Office.
  4. Confirm the $15 driver's-license change charge or the correct non-driver-ID fee.
  5. Contact the Kentucky county clerk about any vehicle title and registration mismatch.
  6. Update voter registration when permitted by election deadlines.
  7. Determine your correct passport form and separately update employment and financial records.
