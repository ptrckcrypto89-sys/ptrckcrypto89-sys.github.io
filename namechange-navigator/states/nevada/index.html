Nevada marriage name change: verified state and federal process

Nevada requires you to update Social Security before the DMV. DMV will match the legal name to SSA records and recommends at least two business days after SSA processing. A certified county marriage certificate, not the license issued before the ceremony, is required as marriage evidence. A name outside the types recognized from the marriage document may require a court order.

1. Order a certified Nevada marriage certificate from the county

Order from the County Recorder where your marriage certificate was recorded, except Clark County (Las Vegas), where the County Clerk maintains marriage certificates. The ceremony officiant has up to 10 business days to record the marriage, and the certified record may not be available until filing is complete. Nevada state vital records does not issue the certified marriage certificates; its separate historical verification/search costs $10 per search and does not include a certified marriage certificate. Certified copy prices vary by county. For marriage outside Nevada, contact the issuing jurisdiction.

Nevada state marriage record custodians · County recorder certified-copy process · NRS 122.055 certification distinction

2. Change your Social Security name first

Use SSA's name-change service, supplying an original or issuing-authority-certified marriage record and required identity/status evidence. This correction is free. Some applicants can begin online; other applications need documents or an SSA visit. The Nevada DMV's current guidance expressly says it cannot process your Nevada license or ID name correction until the updated SSA information is electronically verified. Permit at least two business days for SSA systems to update before your DMV appointment; a new SSA card by itself does not replace the certified marriage evidence.

SSA free name correction · SSA accepted documents · Nevada DMV SSA-first verification

3. Correct Nevada driver license or ID in person

Visit a Nevada DMV office in person with your current driver license/ID, an original or certified marriage certificate or other legal-name document, and completed DMV Form 002. The DMV uses the exact married name on the certified marriage certificate. If the certificate does not list a married name, DMV guidance allows specified surname options: taking the spouse's surname, hyphenating the surnames, or using the prior surname as a middle name and spouse's surname as the last name. A first-name change generally needs another legally sufficient document. REAL ID name chains may require proof of each intervening legal-name change.

The current DMV name-change transaction fees are $8.50 for a noncommercial driver license, $7.50 for an ID card, and $12.50 for a commercial driver license. The Nevada driver's handbook advises giving the DMV notice of an address or legal-name change within 30 days. Nevada Statutes § 483.375 directs a licensee to request a full legal-name correction using an original or certified legal document; apply promptly rather than delaying based on a different state's deadline.

4. Update Nevada vehicle registration, insurance and title as appropriate

Nevada DMV states a title update for the same owner's surname change is optional but recommended. The practical first steps are to obtain updated Nevada insurance using the exact corrected owner name, then bring the certified marriage document, insurance proof, and existing vehicle registration to the DMV for a registration correction. If you choose to correct the title, bring the existing paper title if held. For a mere same-person surname correction, the DMV's Registration and Title Guide states that the $8.25 change-of-ownership title processing fee should not be assessed. Other title and registration issuance fees may still apply; do not confuse a same-owner name change with adding or removing vehicle owners.

5. Correct Nevada voter registration and 2026 election name

Nevada permits ongoing voter record updates and same-day registration, subject to identification and ballot rules. The federal Election Assistance Commission's Nevada schedule permits online registration through Election Day, but new registrants within 14 days of an election must vote in person and present qualifying Nevada or tribal ID. Name changes are distinct from ordinary online updates: Clark County's official elections office says online same-day name changes are unavailable during the final same-day period; make that name correction in person at an early voting site or Election Day vote center. Other Nevada counties may have different locations and requirements; verify directly with the county registrar. For the November 3, 2026 general election, confirm the current eligible voting method and identification before voting.

6. Correct passport details if needed

The State Department bases the passport form on the passport issuance date, when the legal name changed, and renewal eligibility. DS-5504 can apply to name changes within its first-year issuance window; DS-82 or DS-11 may apply instead. Supply the certified marriage certificate and required passport/photo/fees as instructed; confirm travel and booking names before mailing your passport.

7. Update remaining accounts after government ID corrections

Update employer/payroll tax records, banks, lenders, insurance plans, property records, education and professional credentials. Provide only the certified records each service actually needs. Keep copies of the official name-change evidence for later identity checks.

When Nevada requires an adult court name-change petition

Nevada courts specifically say a typical marriage-based last-name change does not require a court order, because a certified marriage certificate can establish the change. For other names or situations lacking acceptable marriage proof, petition the district court where you live under NRS 41.270–41.290. Submit the state's adult petition and accompanying forms. Nevada requires disclosure of felony convictions and, when applicable, fingerprints. Most adult petition notices must be published once in a newspaper; publication is not required for a name change to conform to gender identity, and a court may waive publication in safety-related cases. The court generally waits at least 10 days after publication before issuing an order without a hearing. County court fees and publication costs vary.

Nevada preparation checklist

  1. Get a certified county marriage certificate after recording (Clark County uses the County Clerk).
  2. Correct the Social Security record first and allow at least two business days before your Nevada DMV visit.
  3. Bring DMV Form 002, current credential, and certified evidence to change the driver license or ID in person.
  4. Update the matching vehicle insurance and registration; decide whether to amend the optional same-owner title.
  5. Check Nevada voter name-update rules, especially in-person late-cycle updates in Clark County.
  6. Choose the appropriate passport process and update employer, bank and insurance records.
