Georgia marriage name change: step-by-step agency guide

This guide covers changing the name shown on records following a marriage supported by an eligible certified marriage document. Other name changes, including changes not established by that document, may require a separate court order. Always verify requirements with the issuing agency before submitting.

1. Obtain a certified Georgia marriage record

Request a certified marriage certificate or application from the government office holding your record. Georgia's State Office of Vital Records has marriage applications and certificates for the period June 1952 through August 1996; for most marriages outside that period, contact the county probate court where the marriage license was issued. For marriages in another state, contact the issuing jurisdiction. The state vital-records office lists a $10 search fee including a certified record when found, plus $5 for additional copies ordered at the same time; county fees can differ. Keep a certified copy available for each agency that needs to inspect or retain one.

Georgia Department of Public Health — marriage certificates and applications · Georgia Department of Public Health — fee schedule

2. Update your name with the Social Security Administration

Use the SSA's official name-change process to apply for a corrected Social Security record and replacement card. Depending on your eligibility, you may be able to begin online or need an appointment. Complete the SSA update before going to Georgia DDS when practical: DDS electronically checks your name, birth date, and Social Security number against SSA records, and a discrepancy can delay verification. Do not assume a fixed number of hours guarantees the records have synchronized.

SSA — change your name · Georgia DDS — SSA record matching requirements

3. Change your Georgia driver license or ID in person within 60 days

Georgia DDS states that after a name change by marriage or another legal action, you must obtain a license with the correct name within 60 days. Name changes must be handled in person at a DDS Customer Service Center. Bring your current credential and an original or certified name-change document. DDS accepts appropriate certified marriage-license applications, marriage licenses, state-issued marriage certificates, or court orders. If you married in Georgia on or after November 1, 1982 and want a hyphenated surname, DDS requires a certified copy of the marriage-license application reflecting the selected hyphenated name. DDS describes one free replacement license during a license term; other transactions or extra replacements may involve fees. Review the REAL ID document checklist and local service-center requirements first.

Georgia DDS — name-change instructions and 60-day rule · Georgia DDS — REAL ID and acceptable documents

4. Update vehicle title with your county tag office if applicable

After your Georgia license or ID is corrected, the Georgia Department of Revenue directs vehicle owners to request the same change on their title at the county tag office. For an in-person request, have the current Georgia title, updated valid Georgia driver license or ID, and certified marriage certificate or other legal name-change document. The state also publishes a by-mail process using Form MV-1, the current title, a copy of the updated Georgia credential, and certified name-change evidence. Ask your county office about liens, co-owners, updated registration and applicable title fees.

Georgia Department of Revenue — change name on title

5. Update Georgia voter registration

Georgia's Secretary of State says the simplest way to change your legal name on an existing voter registration is to submit a new voter-registration application; the county registrar will update your existing record. Georgia also provides online services to maintain registration. Check your resulting record using My Voter Page, particularly before an election, and review election deadlines separately.

Georgia Secretary of State — name changes and voter registration FAQ · Georgia My Voter Page

6. Update U.S. passport and private records

The correct U.S. passport form depends on when your passport was issued and when the legal name change occurred. The State Department explains the conditions for DS-5504, DS-82 and DS-11; do not automatically select one form for every marriage. After government identity records are correct, independently update employer/payroll, financial institutions, insurance, schools, professional licenses, healthcare and travel accounts. This website is a personal progress tracker; it does not submit applications or store documents.

Georgia preparation checklist

  1. Get a government-certified marriage certificate or application from the proper county probate court or state vital-records office.
  2. Update SSA records and confirm your name matches before DDS verification.
  3. Visit a Georgia DDS Customer Service Center within 60 days with the required original or certified evidence.
  4. If applicable, correct the vehicle title through the county tag office after updating your Georgia identification.
  5. Update voter registration through the Georgia Secretary of State process and verify the record.
  6. Confirm the appropriate passport form; update payroll, banking and other private records.
