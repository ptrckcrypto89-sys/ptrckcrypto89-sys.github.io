Arkansas step-by-step name-change checklist

1. Obtain legal evidence of the marriage

Arkansas driver-license law lists an original or certified recorded marriage license, court order, divorce decree or another acceptable legal document as proof of a name change. The Arkansas Department of Health offers a separate marriage-record coupon at $10 per copy; ask Driver Services whether a coupon meets its rules before ordering it as your only document.

Arkansas Department of Health: Marriage record requests ↗

2. Correct Social Security

Follow SSA's name-change process and keep a record of accepted documentation. Arkansas DFA lists Social Security identity evidence among driver-license requirements, but its site does not establish a universal processing delay for name changes.

SSA name correction ↗

3. Notify Driver Services within 10 days

Arkansas Code § 27-16-506 requires notifying Driver Services in writing of the former and new name and license number within 10 days after a name change. The law also requires original or certified official documentary evidence to correct the name on the driver's license. DFA's required-documents instructions discuss linking prior and present legal names.

Arkansas Code § 27-16-506 ↗ · DFA driver license requirements ↗

4. Confirm whether the vehicle title must be corrected

Arkansas DFA lists legal name-change evidence (including marriage documentation) among supporting paperwork for certain title transactions. Ask your State Revenue Office which title or registration transaction applies, especially if a lienholder is involved. The general duplicate-title fee should not be assumed to be the legal name-correction fee.

Arkansas DFA Office of Motor Vehicle ↗

5. Update voter registration

Arkansas Secretary of State instructs voters whose legal name changed through marriage to fill out the name-change portion of a voter registration application and submit it to the county clerk. Check the applicable deadline and confirm that the former-name voter record has been updated.

Arkansas voter-registration name-change FAQ ↗

6. Address passport, employment and accounts

If you hold a U.S. passport, check the State Department's name-change process. Update payroll records and bank, insurance, vehicle finance and other accounts as relevant.

Passport name change ↗

Before the Arkansas DFA visit

  1. Obtain the original or certified recorded marriage license required for legal-name evidence.
  2. Complete the SSA identity-name update appropriate to your situation.
  3. Notify the Arkansas Office of Driver Services of the changed name within 10 days.
  4. Bring identity and prior-name linking documents; check the latest DFA list.
  5. Verify the exact driver's license or ID reissue fee in advance.
  6. Confirm any affected vehicle title or registration corrections.
  7. Update voter registration through the county clerk.
  8. Resolve passport, employer and financial account records.
