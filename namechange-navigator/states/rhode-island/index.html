Rhode Island marriage name change: state and federal steps

State-specific agency guidance: This guide explains what a recorded marriage document can establish and how to correct government records in the proper order. Procedures for a name not supported by the marriage record may require a separate court order. Fees and local processing arrangements can change; review issuing-agency instructions before submitting an application.

1. Get the correct certified marriage record

Request a government-certified marriage record through Rhode Island's Center for Vital Records or the authorized city/town clerk. The Department of Health says records for marriages from 2022 onward are available at any municipality; older events generally require the municipality associated with the event or residence. Eligibility and identity proof apply. Confirm current copy charges with the issuing office.

Rhode Island Health certified marriage records

2. Correct your legal name with Social Security

Apply for a corrected Social Security card using an original or issuing-agency-certified marriage certificate and any required identity, citizenship or immigration evidence. The federal SSA legal-name correction is FREE. Some people can begin online; others must send documents or visit SSA. Retain the certified marriage record for other agencies; SSA's free update does not automatically change your DMV, bank, passport or vehicle title records. Follow the state-specific license sequencing noted in the next section.

3. Correct the driver license or state ID

First update your name with SSA and allow at least 24 hours for the federal record to update. Bring the current Rhode Island license, signed LI-1 License/ID/Permit Application, and certified legal-name evidence to a DMV branch; AAA handles only certain middle-name or address changes, not full married-surname updates. Rhode Island lists $28.50 for an updated or duplicate license, including the technology surcharge. The 10-day notice on the DMV's page refers specifically to address changes; do not imply it applies to marriage-name changes without legal support.

4. Correct vehicle title and registration when necessary

Rhode Island DMV says correct the license first, then the registration and title. Bring the new temporary or permanent license, original title, certified marriage record, insurance and a notarized TR-1 registration application. A lender may need to release the title for processing. When the title is in your possession, DMV instructs signing the old name as seller and new name as buyer solely for the same-owner name correction; do not use that instruction for a genuine sale.

5. Update voter registration or voting identification

Use Rhode Island's Voter Information Center or local board of canvassers to update the name on an existing registration. Ordinary new-voter registration closes 30 days before an election, but the state's regulation separately recognizes a signed voter-affirmation form at the polling place for an already-registered voter changing a name or address. These routes are not interchangeable with late first-time voter registration.

6. Choose the appropriate passport correction form

The U.S. State Department determines whether Form DS-5504, DS-82 or DS-11 is suitable based on passport issue date, timing of your legal name change and renewal eligibility. A qualifying first-year correction can have no standard application fee; that rule does not apply to all applicants. Submit the proper certified marriage record, existing passport, new photo and applicable fees exactly as the agency directs. Confirm the name on travel reservations before mailing a passport.

7. Update employer, finance, insurance and other records

After official IDs are corrected, align payroll and tax records, banks and loans, medical coverage, auto and property insurance, benefits, real estate records, professional licenses, schools and memberships. Keep the certified marriage certificate available to establish the link between names. Do not upload Social Security numbers or identity documents to this independent website.

When a Rhode Island court-order name change is different

If the chosen name cannot be established by the legally certified marriage document, obtain the current name-change petition requirements from the Rhode Island courts rather than treating an informal certificate as a court order.

Marriage evidence ordinarily supports only the new name legally authorized by that recorded marriage. If a court order is required, petition fees, hearings, publication, confidentiality and other requirements can vary. A vehicle title or replacement license is NOT itself a judicial name-change order.

Rhode Island preparation checklist

  1. Locate the legally recorded marriage certificate and request the proper certified copy.
  2. Correct the Social Security legal-name record using issuing-authority evidence.
  3. Follow the Rhode Island license/ID process, document requirements and any stated deadline.
  4. Correct vehicle title and registration where necessary, coordinating with lienholders.
  5. Update the voter record or voter identification under the state's actual election rules.
  6. Review passport form eligibility; update payroll, banks and insurance records.
