Colorado marriage name-change steps

1. Obtain the recorded, certified marriage certificate

Ask the county clerk and recorder that issued and recorded the marriage license for a certified copy of the marriage record. Confirm that you are ordering an official certified marriage certificate or recorded license rather than a state verification or decorative copy that may not prove your legal name change. County fees and ordering methods vary.

For example, El Paso County publishes a $1 certified document-copy fee for its own marriage records, but this is not a statewide fee. Adams County instructs newly married residents to obtain certified copies for their Social Security name update.

El Paso County clerk — Marriage records and certified copies · Adams County clerk — After the ceremony

2. Update Social Security before the Colorado DMV

Request a corrected Social Security card/name record through SSA using the required marriage and identity evidence. Some applicants can use an online SSA workflow; others need an appointment. Colorado DMV requires the SSA name update to be recorded before the DMV visit for applicants with a Social Security number. DMV describes an approximately 24–48-hour SSA processing interval, but verify the completed record rather than relying on an estimate.

SSA — Official name-change application steps

3. Book a driver-license or ID name-change appointment

Colorado DMV requires renewal in the new legal name within 30 days of a marriage-related name change. The name update requires an in-person appointment. If you already have a Colorado credential, use the DMV's Driver License Renewal appointment category. Bring original or certified name-change documents, existing identity evidence and any legal-name records necessary to connect earlier names. Photocopies and ordinary notarizations are not substitutes for agency-certified identity documents.

DMV's document checklist covers identity/lawful status, legal name, Social Security information, and Colorado residence. For REAL ID, the DMV generally lists two proofs of residence; different credential and immigration categories may have additional requirements. Check the exact requirement and fee before the appointment.

DMV — Name change and appointment instructions · DMV — Official document checklist · DMV — Current fee table

4. Update your car title or registration when necessary

Colorado says title and registration name changes are handled by county motor vehicle offices, separate from the state driver-license office. If you hold the title, the state describes changing the owner name as a documented title transfer from the former name to the new name. Bring name-change documents and secure/verifiable ID. If there is a lien, contact the lienholder first.

Colorado DMV lists $7.20 as a title-transfer fee, but a different transaction classification may apply to an isolated correction. Confirm the procedure and cost with your county office rather than assuming every name update costs the same.

Colorado DMV — Title names and lienholder procedure · DMV — County motor vehicle services

5. Submit a PAPER voter-registration name change

The Colorado Secretary of State says name changes to voter registration must be submitted on a paper registration form, not online. Get the official name-change form, follow the county submission directions, and verify election-specific filing deadlines. Updating an address online is not the same process as changing the registered name.

Colorado Secretary of State — Change my name · Go Vote Colorado — Name-change form

6. Passport and remaining identity records

For an existing passport, check whether you qualify to use DS-5504, DS-82 or DS-11; the correct process depends on when the passport was issued, when the name was changed and whether you qualify for renewal. Review payroll, banks, insurance, mortgages, professional licenses and travel accounts separately.

U.S. State Department — Passport name change

Printable Colorado task checklist

  1. Confirm your county and request the correct certified recorded marriage document.
  2. Submit the SSA legal-name change and confirm it has been recorded.
  3. Schedule an in-person DMV legal-name/renewal appointment within 30 days of the marriage-related name change.
  4. Assemble original certified marriage proof, name chain, identity, and residence documents.
  5. Confirm the transaction-specific DMV fee before arriving.
  6. Ask your county motor vehicle office or title lienholder about needed title corrections.
  7. Submit the paper Colorado voter-registration name-change form.
  8. Apply for the appropriate passport correction and update any name-linked private records.
