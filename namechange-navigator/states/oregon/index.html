Oregon marriage name change: government and federal checklist

Oregon DMV verifies new legal names with Social Security before a new credential is issued. For a standard Oregon license/ID, DMV can in limited situations verify a prior SSA name using additional legal proof, while for REAL ID both the full legal name and Social Security number must match current SSA records. An ordinary DMV name change requires an appointment or in-office standby visit with original or government-certified name-change evidence.

1. Order a certified Oregon marriage certificate

Request an Oregon Center for Health Statistics certified marriage record after the county has filed the completed marriage form. The Oregon Health Authority's base record search/certified-copy charge is $25. Mail and drop-box orders cost the $25 base amount; in-person requests carry an extra $3 identity authentication charge ($28 total). Online vendor orders currently add a $7 expedite fee and $15.50 vendor/ID review fee ($47.50 total). Certificates are restricted to eligible requesters for 50 years after the marriage. County-issued certified records may be available before the state fully registers the marriage, usually around a month after paperwork submission, but Oregon Health Authority warns that county-certified marriage records may not be accepted for passport applications. For federal passport needs, order a state-issued vital-record certificate as the agency specifies. If married elsewhere, contact the original issuing jurisdiction.

2. Submit the Social Security name correction first

Use SSA's legal-name-change service with original or issuing-authority-certified marriage evidence and required identity/citizenship or lawful-status documents. This update is free; eligible applicants can start online, while others use SSA's document or office process. Oregon DMV attempts to validate the name and SSN against SSA records. To obtain a REAL ID name correction, the agency requires an exact SSA current-name match. Correct the SSA record before presenting the DMV documents to avoid mismatch delays.

3. Replace the Oregon driver license, permit or ID at an office

Oregon DMV directs married-name applicants to make an appointment or visit for standby service, bring an original or issuing-authority-certified marriage license/Certificate of Record of Marriage or other legal court name evidence, proof of identity and address, and pay the credential's applicable replacement fee. Oregon's current fee chart lists a $30 standard Class C license or permit replacement, $40 ID-card replacement, and $30 additional REAL ID charge for applicable REAL ID replacements. Bring any certified documents connecting old names if a birth record or other primary ID still uses a previous surname. The new credential is mailed, with an interim paper receipt issued at the office. The reviewed official name-change page does not provide a universal 10- or 30-day deadline after marriage; do not copy a different state's statutory deadline.

4. Update vehicle title and registration separately

Changing your Oregon driver's license does not automatically correct the vehicle title or registration. For an ownership record under a prior married surname, DMV instructs you to complete a title application noting that your name has changed, attach the existing title, pay the appropriate vehicle title fee, and file by mail or in a DMV office. The state's present title fee depends on vehicle type and fuel efficiency. For passenger vehicles it ranges from $101 for 0–19 MPG, $106 for 20–39 MPG, $116 for 40+ MPG, to $192 for electric; different vehicle categories and lien circumstances can change the fee. This is different from a DMV typo correction (where the DMV misspelled the owner's name), which is free. Do not confuse correcting a same-person married name with transferring ownership after a sale; a sale's late title-transfer penalty has a different trigger.

5. Correct Oregon voter name using a signed paper registration form

The Oregon Secretary of State explicitly states that a legal-name change must be updated by submitting a new paper voter-registration form, even when the voter has a DMV number and can otherwise update certain voter details online. Submit the signed paper form to the county election office, including the information needed to find the old registration. Existing registered Oregon voters may update their information until 8 p.m. on Election Day and still cast a ballot, but updates close to election day might require ballot issuance at the county elections office. New voter registrations have a different deadline: 21 days before election day, which for the November 3, 2026 election was October 13, 2026. Oregon will mail ballots beginning October 14 and accepts ballot drop-offs until 8 p.m. on November 3. Do not assume an online name-only update works through My Vote.

6. Correct your U.S. passport, where required

Use the State Department's form selection: DS-5504 is available only to qualifying changes within a recent one-year passport issuance and name-change window. Other cases use DS-82 if renewal eligible or DS-11 for new/in-person filing. Submit an acceptable original/certified marriage record, passport, photo and applicable fees according to the form. In particular, the Oregon Health Authority cautions that a county-issued certified record may not be adequate for some passport applications; check the federal evidence rule before submitting an Oregon marriage record.

7. Finish employer, insurer and bank record updates

Correct payroll and tax forms, bank and credit accounts, property interests, professional licenses, health records, utilities, retirement accounts and insurance. Retain the official name-linking evidence for institutions that require more than a new photo ID.

When Oregon needs a court name-change judgment

Where your chosen legal name cannot be established by the recorded marriage, file an adult Petition for Change of Name in the Oregon Circuit Court in your county of residence. The Oregon Judicial Department provides official adult identity-change petitions and judgment forms. The court's updated July 2026 instructions require the petition to disclose all previously used names on the appropriate exhibit and declaration. Court fees vary with the requested relief and current court schedule, and privacy protections may apply for Address Confidentiality Program participants. The name judgment becomes effective on entry in the court record; other agencies still need their own update requests. This court process is separate from routine certified-marriage evidence.

Oregon preparation checklist

  1. Get an official state or appropriate county-certified marriage certificate (state mail base $25).
  2. Correct the SSA name record at no charge, particularly for an Oregon REAL ID exact match.
  3. Visit Oregon DMV with official marriage proof; review $30 license or $40 ID replacement and REAL ID surcharge.
  4. Apply separately for a changed-name vehicle title/registration and pay the specific vehicle title fee where needed.
  5. Submit a signed paper voter-registration name update; for existing voters, election-day updates may be possible via county election offices.
  6. Select the correct passport form and update employer, financial and insurance records.
