Nebraska marriage name change: exact agency sequence

Nebraska DMV requires you to update the name on a driver's license, permit, or state ID within 60 days. A certified marriage document connecting your prior and new names ordinarily supports a married-surname change. Changing first or middle names via marriage evidence has extra documentary requirements. A separate Nebraska district-court petition is needed when the marriage record does not legally establish the proposed name.

1. Obtain a certified Nebraska marriage certificate

Request an official certified marriage certificate from the Nebraska Department of Health and Human Services Vital Records. Its official order portal lists a $16 fee per certified marriage certificate, requires a requester to meet the state's proper-purpose eligibility rules, and warns that online orders may take 4–6 weeks to be received by mail. Government agencies may reject ceremony keepsakes and scans; use an issuing-authority-certified document. For a marriage recorded in another state, request that jurisdiction's certified document.

Nebraska DHHS official certificate order and fee

2. Change the name on the SSA record

Apply to the Social Security Administration for a corrected Social Security card using your certified marriage document and required identity/status evidence. SSA does not charge for a legal-name correction. Certain applications can begin online; others require an office appointment or document submission. Allow the new name to be reflected in SSA records before visiting Nebraska DMV to reduce mismatch problems, but do not delay beyond the DMV's 60-day deadline.

3. Update your Nebraska license, permit or ID within 60 days

Visit a Nebraska Driver Licensing Office in person; the DMV says name changes are not available online. Bring your existing credential, one form of acceptable identification showing name and birth date, and certified evidence such as a marriage license showing the link between prior and new surnames. If changing a name component besides the last name using the marriage license, the DMV requires the complete legal name before and after marriage on that license. Complete a DMV data form; address documentation may be required if not previously verified, and the DMV will take a new photograph.

The DMV's current Class O replacement fee is $11 plus a $5 security fee, totaling $16; other classes and state ID card rates can differ. A temporary receipt is issued, and the permanent replacement generally arrives by mail in 14 business days. Your previous credential becomes invalid after replacement.

4. Correct Nebraska vehicle title if you own a vehicle

Visit the county Motor Vehicle Office with a completed Application for Certificate of Title, the existing title if paper, certified proof of legal name change, and the $10 new-title fee. If a lien is on the paper title, ask the lender to forward the original title to the county. For an electronic title with a lien, complete the vehicle-title change request through the lender, then present the lender-approved request to the county with the application and fee. The DMV distinguishes an owner's name correction from adding/removing an owner, which has separate consent requirements.

5. Update voter registration and verify 2026 election deadlines

Nebraska's Secretary of State requires voters to re-register when their name changes. Its online voter portal allows name changes using a valid Nebraska driver license or state ID; mail registration is also possible. For the November 3, 2026 general election, online/mail changes needed to be submitted by Friday, October 16, 2026, and the in-person county election office deadline was Friday, October 23, 2026 at 6 p.m.. Those standard pre-election windows have now closed. However, already-registered voters with a name mismatch may still change their voter registration at the polling place on Election Day and cast a regular ballot under Nebraska § 32-914.01, subject to applicable photo-ID rules and identity verification. This is not a way for an unregistered person to enroll after the cutoff.

6. Correct U.S. passport, if needed

Follow the State Department's passport name-change decision tool. DS-5504 can apply within its one-year issuance and change window, while DS-82 or DS-11 applies to different renewal or new-passport circumstances. Required evidence typically includes the issued or certified marriage record, the existing passport, photo, and applicable fee. Confirm travel arrangements before mailing a passport.

7. Finish non-government records

Update employer payroll and tax reporting, insurance, financial accounts, medical records, real estate interests, subscriptions, and professional credentials using the corrected identification and certified marriage certificate as required. Keep your certified evidence securely stored.

When Nebraska requires a court name-change petition

Marriage-related surnames documented in the certificate commonly proceed without a separate court petition. For changes outside the scope of the legal marriage evidence, Nebraska's district court provides an adult petition process. The Nebraska Judicial Branch says the adult petitioner must generally have been a bona fide resident of the filing county for one year and be age 19 or legally emancipated or married. Begin with Form DC 6:9.1, a confidential-information form, and other court instructions. A legal notice must normally run once weekly for four weeks before a hearing; waiver procedures may be available to people at risk. Court filing, publication, and certified-decree costs are separate from license and marriage-record fees.

Nebraska preparation checklist

  1. Obtain the $16 certified Nebraska marriage certificate or the certified document from your marriage state.
  2. Request a no-fee Social Security name update and ensure the new identity record is processed.
  3. Apply in person for a corrected Nebraska license or ID within 60 days, with appropriate certified evidence.
  4. Request the $10 Nebraska title name correction through the county and lender if applicable.
  5. Update voter registration and check the special Election Day name-mismatch procedure if you are already registered.
  6. Choose the proper passport application and correct payroll, banking and insurance names.
