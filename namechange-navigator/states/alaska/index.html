Step-by-step Alaska name update

1. Get your certified Alaska marriage certificate

The Alaska Department of Health lists certified marriage-record copies at $30 for the first copy and $25 for each additional copy. Use the official Vital Records ordering service, confirm eligibility, and retain the original or certified evidence required by subsequent agencies.

Alaska Vital Records orders ↗

2. Change your Social Security record

Start at SSA to determine whether your case can be handled online or requires an office appointment. Alaska DMV's identification guidance instructs applicants to update Social Security before changing the name on a DMV-issued credential.

SSA official name-change process ↗

3. Notify Alaska DMV within 30 days and correct your license or state ID

Alaska DMV cites AS 28.05.071 requiring name or address changes to be reported within 30 days. For a name correction on a license, permit or state ID, submit an Application for Driver License, Permit or State Identification Card (Form D1) and proof of your name change in person. Evidence must be original or certified; photocopies and faxes are not accepted. DMV may require a vision test. If you have changed names multiple times, provide a chain of official documents linking them.

Accepted proof may include a certified Vital Statistics marriage certificate, court order, or other qualifying document listed by Alaska DMV. A divorce decree must establish the new legal name.

Alaska DMV: changing identification details and Form D1 ↗

4. Update a vehicle title if applicable

For a name-only title change without an ownership change, Alaska DMV requires an updated Application for Title and Registration, the original title, and legal proof of the change (such as an original or certified marriage certificate). The title fee is $15. A lienholder may require additional steps; confirm details for your title and registration status.

5. Update Alaska voter registration

The Alaska Division of Elections directs voters to use the online voter registration system or their regional office when a voter record needs updating, including a name change. Check election-specific deadlines, generally 30 days before an election for registration updates.

6. Finish federal and everyday records

Check the State Department passport-name-change instructions for your specific circumstances. Update employment/payroll, financial accounts, insurance and other identity records afterward.

Alaska preparation checklist

  1. Request certified marriage proof from Alaska Vital Records if needed.
  2. Process the updated legal name with SSA.
  3. Prepare Form D1, existing credential and an original/certified proof of name change.
  4. Notify Alaska DMV within 30 days and verify licensing office requirements.
  5. Correct the vehicle title where applicable.
  6. Update Alaska voter records by the applicable election deadline.
  7. Review passport, employer, banking and insurance records.
