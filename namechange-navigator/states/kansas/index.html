Kansas marriage name change: step-by-step guide

For updating an adult’s name after marriage using a government-issued marriage record. A routine married-surname update is different from petitioning for a different legal name that your marriage document does not support. Agencies may request a judicial order for a change outside what the certified marriage evidence establishes.

1. Obtain a government-certified marriage record

If your marriage took place in Kansas, the completed marriage license is first filed with the clerk of the district court in the county that issued it; the court then forwards the record to Kansas Department of Health and Environment (KDHE) Office of Vital Statistics. Order a certified marriage certificate after recording. The state currently lists $20 per certified copy for mail or walk-in ordering; online or expedited options can add service fees. Do not substitute a decorative ceremony certificate or a courthouse “duplicate marriage license” for an agency-issued certified certificate when Kansas Revenue requires proof of the change. If married outside Kansas, order the official certified record from the issuing jurisdiction.

KDHE certified marriage records and ordering options

2. Update Social Security before applying at Kansas driver licensing

Start a corrected Social Security card request using SSA's change-name portal; submit original or agency-certified evidence, identity documents, and citizenship or immigration evidence when required. SSA may allow an online start, with an appointment or Form SS-5 if needed. Kansas Revenue expressly requires that your SSA name update occur at least 24 hours before your Kansas credential name-change transaction. Avoid appearing at the DMV before this synchronization window.

SSA document checklist · Kansas Revenue's explicit 24-hour rule

3. Replace your Kansas driver's license, permit or ID

Visit a Kansas driver-license station with (a) your existing Kansas credential, (b) a piece of mail showing your Kansas residential address (it may use your prior name), and (c) a certified government-issued marriage certificate or other accepted legal-name change document. Kansas Revenue lists a $16 name-change replacement license fee or $12 for a non-driver ID. Name change is handled as a replacement, not the lower-cost generic “change” transaction. Applicants changing their current name from their proof of lawful presence may also need the certified chain of documents connecting those names.

Deadline check: Reviewed Kansas licensing material establishes the 24-hour SSA lead time, not a verified universal 10-day or 30-day post-marriage deadline for replacement of a credential. Do not confuse other address-change requirements with a name-change deadline.

4. Correct vehicle title records through the county treasurer

If you have a Kansas vehicle title, the Kansas Division of Vehicles instructs owners to take the current title and a copy of the marriage license or court order to the county treasurer’s motor-vehicle office to update the titled owner's legal name. Record the name or names to appear on the replacement title in the title assignment space following the agency's instructions. If a lender holds the title, obtain the lienholder's completed, signed and notarized Form TR-128, Lienholder's Consent to Transfer Ownership, with the marriage evidence. Kansas currently lists a $10 title fee for a legal-name change; taxes or other charges can differ if you are also changing ownership. Do not treat a spouse addition or title transfer as only a name correction.

Kansas Division of Vehicles: title name changes and Form TR-128

5. Re-register to vote under the new name

The Kansas Secretary of State directs voters to re-register whenever their name changes. Online registration is available to people with a valid Kansas driver license or non-driver ID; otherwise submit a paper registration to your county election officer. Kansas voter registration closes 21 days before an election. For the November 3, 2026 general election, the posted name-and-address update deadline was October 13, 2026 (now past). Check the election office for any legally available options if you missed an applicable cutoff, and verify your updated registration before voting.

6. Select the passport application route based on your two dates

The U.S. State Department generally allows Form DS-5504 when both the passport issuance and legal name change occurred within the last year. Send the appropriate certified marriage evidence, current passport and photo according to the form instructions; standard correction can be no-fee in the qualifying window. Otherwise confirm whether you are eligible for a DS-82 mail renewal or must use DS-11 in person. Do not assume a pending passport name change updates airline bookings automatically.

U.S. passport name corrections

7. Keep your Kansas birth certificate unchanged for routine married-surname changes

KDHE specifically explains that a person who gets married does not need to change their birth certificate; the original shows their birth name and the marriage record provides proof of the later married surname. If the filed marriage certificate contains a genuine recording error, use KDHE's separate amendment procedure instead of requesting a new legal name as a “correction.” KDHE's current guidance describes identity and notarized requests and lists a $20 amendment fee, separate from the cost of a certified copy.

KDHE birth-certificate marriage FAQ · KDHE marriage-record amendment process

8. Update work, financial accounts and other credentials

Once your legal identity records reflect the married surname, individually update your employer's payroll, insurance policies, professional credentials, banking records, taxes, travel profiles and property records. Save certified copies for agencies requiring them, and protect original documents. No website checklist step files an application on your behalf.

When a Kansas court name-change petition is needed

For a requested legal name that cannot be supported by a certified marriage record, ask the relevant agency what evidence is required rather than assuming your marriage itself changes any first or middle name. Kansas district courts have separate adult name-change petition procedures and state-developed form templates under K.S.A. chapter 60, article 14. Court notice requirements and filing charges are not the same as replacing a post-marriage credential; check with the district court clerk before filing. A restoration of a former name in a divorce is a separate procedure.

Kansas preparation checklist

  1. Order the government-certified marriage record from KDHE or the registering jurisdiction.
  2. Complete the corrected SSA record and allow at least 24 hours before Kansas licensing.
  3. Gather Kansas license or ID, address mail, and the certified name-change document.
  4. Budget Kansas's current $16 driver-license or $12 ID name-change fee.
  5. Update any Kansas vehicle title through the county treasurer; coordinate lienholder Form TR-128 when applicable.
  6. Re-register to vote under your new name and check the election deadline.
  7. Select the correct passport form, and complete employer, bank, insurance and other record changes.
