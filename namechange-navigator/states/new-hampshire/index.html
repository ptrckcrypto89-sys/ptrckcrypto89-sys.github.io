New Hampshire marriage name change: complete agency checklist

New Hampshire Saf-C 1011.02 requires a licensee to submit a name-change record correction within 30 days. Use DSMV 30 and marriage documentation to update the DMV. A marriage certificate generally supports an appropriate married-surname correction; a separate probate-court petition may be necessary for a different legal name not established through marriage.

1. Order a certified New Hampshire marriage certificate

Request a certified marriage certificate from the New Hampshire Division of Vital Records Administration or a participating city/town clerk. People requesting vital records must demonstrate a direct and tangible interest and show photographic identification. New Hampshire's official search charge is $15, including a certified copy if the record is located; additional certified copies of the same event purchased together are $10 each. Marriage records dating from 1960 generally appear through participating town/city vital-record systems; for older records, check the clerk where the license was issued. Certified records from marriages outside New Hampshire must be requested from the original issuing state or county. Do not use an uncertified scan for a legal identity change.

2. Correct Social Security identification

Use the Social Security Administration's name-change service, providing original or certified marriage evidence and the required identity, citizenship or immigration records. An SSA name correction is free. Start online where eligible or submit the documents through SSA. Once the new name is recorded, continue with DMV and other agencies; avoid having conflicting names on your credentials.

SSA change name service · SSA document requirements

3. Apply for the replacement New Hampshire driver license or ID within 30 days

NH Administrative Code Saf-C 1011.02 requires a licensed person to complete and submit Form DSMV 30 within 30 days of a legal name change. The DMV accepts evidence such as a marriage certificate, court name-change petition/order, divorce decree, passport or adoption decree, as appropriate. Visit a New Hampshire DMV office in person to provide the documentary proof, surrender the existing driver license, and obtain a replacement bearing the new name. The DSMV 30 form explicitly says the request updates DMV data across registration, driver license, title and other records, so ask which replacement documents you need before processing multiple transactions.

The DMV's duplicate driver license or non-driver ID form DSMV 637 lists a $10 duplicate issuance fee for either card. This is the duplicate-card schedule, not the price of first issuance or normal renewal; confirm the exact transaction charge with the DMV. Make sure to distinguish form DSMV 30 (the name-change record update) from DSMV 637 (the duplicate credential application).

Saf-C 1011.02 30-day name-change rule · DSMV 30 record change · DSMV 637 duplicate credential $10 fee

4. Correct title or registration records for vehicles

Because NH's DSMV 30 is intended to update DMV records including registration and title, use it to request correction of the owner name in state motor-vehicle records. The New Hampshire DMV Title Handbook distinguishes a married or divorced owner's legal surname correction from a transfer of vehicle ownership and states that no title application fee is charged for a pure legal-name correction. This does not cover adding or removing owners, buying or selling the vehicle, or other transactions that incur title fees. Bring certified name-change proof and ask the clerk whether the printed title and registration need reissuance, particularly if a lender holds the title.

DMV DSMV 30, all-record name updates · NH Title Handbook: no legal-name correction title fee

5. Update New Hampshire voter registration

Review your name on the voter checklist with your city or town clerk and complete an updated voter-registration application if necessary. New Hampshire permits regular in-person registration through the last town clerk or supervisors of the checklist meeting, typically 6–13 days before election day. Eligible residents can also register to vote at their polling place on Election Day with the necessary proof of identity, age, U.S. citizenship, and domicile. For November 3, 2026, confirm local clerk meeting times and the correct town/ward polling place rather than relying on another state's early-registration cutoff. New Hampshire's mailed absentee-registration procedures apply to qualified absentee registrants and have separate deadlines.

6. Correct U.S. passport, if applicable

Follow the State Department's name-change form decision. Under its eligibility rules, DS-5504 may apply to certain first-year name corrections without the usual application fee, while DS-82 or DS-11 applies to renewal or in-person submissions. Send the current or previous passport, certified marriage record, photo and applicable fees exactly as the selected form requires.

7. Update other agencies and private accounts

Update payroll and tax records, benefits, banks and creditors, insurance, property records, professional registrations and utilities. Use the matching certified marriage and government identity records each organization requires.

When New Hampshire requires a probate-court order

If the legal name you want is not established by the marriage certificate, use the New Hampshire Circuit Court Probate Division name-change process. NH law RSA 547:3-i authorizes probate courts to consider name-change petitions. The court's adult name-change electronic filing guide identifies Petition for Change of Name Form NHJB-2175-FPe and a confidential-information sheet. The court charges a separate filing fee, which its example instructions have listed at $130, but confirm the current court fee schedule because court charges may change. Specific cases involving incarceration, probation/parole, sex-offender registration or certain violent crimes must satisfy the additional legal criteria in §547:3-i and may require service on agencies or more evidence. This is distinct from a regular marriage-based surname update.

New Hampshire preparation checklist

  1. Order a certified marriage certificate ($15 initial vital-record search/certified copy).
  2. Submit the free SSA legal-name correction and verify the identity record.
  3. File DSMV 30 and replace the NH license/ID within 30 days; bring certified marriage evidence.
  4. Use the same state DMV record correction for vehicle registration/title and ask about document reissuance.
  5. Verify voter checklist name with your city/town clerk and prepare election-day registration evidence if needed.
  6. Review passport form eligibility and update employer, banking and insurance names.
