Florida name change after marriage: step-by-step agency guide

For an ordinary marriage-based surname change supported by your government-issued marriage record. If your desired change is not established by that record, you may need a court order instead. Procedures, fees and individual document requirements can vary.

1. Get an official certified marriage certificate

If you married in Florida, obtain a government-certified record from the county clerk that recorded your marriage or Florida's Bureau of Vital Statistics. Statewide records may take approximately 60 days after the ceremony to reach the Bureau, so for a recent marriage contact the issuing county clerk directly. If you married outside Florida, order the certified record from the jurisdiction that recorded the marriage. Budget for certified copies; fees vary by ordering channel.

Florida Department of Health — certified marriage records and ordering methods

2. Update your name with Social Security before Florida DMV

Use the Social Security Administration's name-change process to request an updated Social Security record and replacement card. Depending on eligibility, you may start online or need an appointment. SSA generally requires original or issuing-agency-certified evidence, not ordinary photocopies. Complete the SSA update before visiting Florida's driver-license office; allow at least 48 hours for agency records to match, and confirm locally if more time is necessary.

SSA — change name · SSA — required documents

3. Replace your Florida driver license or ID within 30 days

Florida requires a name change on your driver license or state ID within 30 days. Visit a driver-license service center or county tax collector that handles licensing. Bring your current credential plus an original or certified government-issued marriage certificate, and any connected documents needed to show the complete name history. Check the official REAL ID evidence checklist for your citizenship/immigration category and whether address proofs are required. Name changes normally require in-person verification; confirm appointment rules and transaction fees with your county office. The state may assign a new credential number following a legal-name change.

Florida Highway Safety and Motor Vehicles — name and address changes · FLHSMV — identity and REAL ID document checklist

4. Update Florida vehicle title and registration when applicable

Florida's name/address-change guidance also directs owners to update vehicle title and registration within 30 days after a legal name change. Start with your county tax collector or license-plate agency; requirements can depend on electronic/paper title status, a lien and the registered owners. Have your updated identification, proof of the name change and vehicle records available, and confirm any title/replacement charges with the office.

FLHSMV — motor vehicle name updates · FLHSMV — titles and liens

5. Correct Florida voter registration

Florida's Division of Elections permits voters to update the name on an existing registration through the state's online registration service, a printed application or the county Supervisor of Elections. Confirm that the voter record matches your updated Florida driver license or ID number if one was reissued. Check upcoming-election registration and party-change deadlines separately.

Florida Division of Elections — update a voter record · Florida official online registration

6. Correct your passport and other important accounts

Passport name-change paperwork depends on both when your current passport was issued and when the legal name change occurred. The U.S. Department of State explains when to use DS-5504, DS-82 or DS-11. Coordinate travel reservations with the name shown on the passport you will use. Then update employer/payroll, banking, insurance, medical, professional-license and school records directly with each organization; this website does not transmit changes for you.

U.S. Department of State — passport name changes

Florida preparation checklist

  1. Order your certified marriage document from the recording jurisdiction.
  2. Submit SSA name correction; retain confirmation and allow at least 48 hours before the Florida license visit.
  3. Check REAL ID documents and update Florida driver license or ID within 30 days.
  4. Update any affected Florida title and vehicle registration within 30 days.
  5. Correct the name on Florida voter registration; verify a newly issued license number.
  6. Use the applicable federal passport form and notify private institutions.
