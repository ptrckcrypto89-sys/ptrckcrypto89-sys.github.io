Maryland marriage name change: certified records, licensing and voting

This guide covers an adult surname change supported by a certified marriage record. Maryland courts also recognize informal name use and separate judicial orders, but the Maryland MVA and other agencies have specific evidence and Social Security matching rules. A marriage certificate does not automatically update government or banking records.

1. Order the Maryland certified marriage certificate from the correct agency

The Maryland Department of Health Division of Vital Records issues certified marriage records for ceremonies on or after January 1, 2007. For earlier marriages, contact the circuit court in the county of marriage or Maryland State Archives. Either spouse can request their own record; an authorized representative needs the department's specified signed and notarized authorization, and an attorney needs proof of representation. The official state price for a certified marriage certificate is $12, with an additional $13 internet-service charge and optional shipping charges for certain ordering channels. Mail orders require the official application, copies of accepted identification, payment and a self-addressed stamped envelope. For an out-of-state marriage obtain the certificate from the state or county that recorded it.

2. Correct the Social Security name record at least 72 hours before MVA

Apply for the updated Social Security card using SSA's official name-change service, submitting a government-certified marriage certificate, acceptable identity document and any other evidence SSA requires. Maryland MVA specifically says to update SSA at least 72 hours before the MVA appointment to allow identity matching; its non-driver ID page notes that it can take 72 hours or longer. If you still have not received the new physical card, bring available SSA proof and verify database processing with MVA before visiting. SSA card issuance is free.

SSA identity and evidence rules · Maryland MVA 72-hour SSA synchronization guidance

3. Correct your Maryland license in person at an MVA branch

Schedule a driver-license name-correction appointment and bring your current Maryland license plus an original or issuing-agency-certified legal-change document such as a marriage certificate, divorce decree, court order or passport displaying the new name. MVA will not accept photocopies for name-change evidence. Present SSA-change proof where possible. Maryland's published corrected non-commercial driver's license fee is $30, while a corrected state photo ID card is also $30. The MVA takes a new photo if needed and mails the new credential. Maryland's 30-day statutory rule on this MVA page addresses residential-address changes; a statewide post-marriage name-change deadline has not been established by the reviewed materials, so do not mislabel the address rule.

4. Correct a Maryland motor vehicle title or registration with Form VR-448

Unlike some states, Maryland explicitly maintains a name-correction transaction for vehicle titles. MDOT MVA directs owners to request a corrected title or registration after a legal name change, ordinarily using Application for Corrected Title Due to a Name Change (Form VR-448), evidence such as the government-certified marriage record, a valid ID, the original title/registration if available and any required payment. Some requests can be mailed, while others require a full-service MVA office. MVA explicitly warns do not order a duplicate title when the name is incorrect; use the correction process. Its current posted fee for a corrected title certificate is $80, distinct from the $40 duplicate-title fee. Contact MVA about lienholder documents or any special ownership changes before submission.

5. Update Maryland voter registration under the new name

The Maryland State Board of Elections allows existing voters to update their legal name using the online voter-registration update service, by mailing a registration application, or by providing the local election board a signed written notice. For the November 3, 2026 gubernatorial general election, the normal advance deadline for registration/name updates was October 13, 2026 at 11:59 p.m., now past. Maryland's official election guidance explains that an eligible voter can provide new information during voting, with procedures determined at the polling place. If you also moved, address changes can affect your precinct or require a provisional ballot; verify directly with the local board instead of assuming a late website entry is processed before Election Day.

6. Choose the correct federal passport change process

The U.S. State Department distinguishes passport issuance and legal-name change timing. Some eligible applicants use DS-5504 if both qualifying dates fall within the last year. Otherwise the correct route may be DS-82 or DS-11, depending on eligibility. Prepare certified marriage evidence, valid photo and existing passport as directed. Fees vary by form and requested expedited service. Check travel deadlines, and keep booked travel names matched to the identity document being used.

7. Complete payroll, property and private account changes

Update employer payroll, tax records, health and car insurance, bank and financial accounts, educational institutions, professional licensing boards and travel accounts separately. The MVA or SSA update does not automatically reconcile unrelated financial and property records.

Maryland: when married-name evidence is insufficient

Maryland courts distinguish common-law use of a name from formal court proceedings. MDOT MVA has a special common-law evidence alternative: where the applicant lacks a marriage certificate, divorce decree or name-change order, it may accept an SSA card and two additional qualifying documents consistently showing the new name, such as tax, bank or voter records, subject to the agency's review. This is not a guaranteed substitute for certified marriage evidence in all contexts. For changes requiring a formal judicial record, Maryland Courts provides Form CC-DR-060 (Petition for Change of Name of an Adult) under Maryland Rule 15-901, with current county circuit-court instructions. Ask the court for its filing charge and service requirements before starting. Do not treat a court petition as mandatory for an ordinary surname update that MVA accepts under a certified marriage certificate.

Maryland preparation checklist

  1. Order the official certified marriage certificate; use MDH for 2007 onward and circuit court/Archives for older marriages.
  2. Correct SSA records and allow at least 72 hours before your Maryland MVA appointment.
  3. Bring old license and an original/certified name-change document to the MVA; confirm the $30 corrected license/ID fee.
  4. For a Maryland vehicle in your name, submit a Form VR-448 title correction; check the $80 corrected title charge.
  5. Update voter registration under your new name and check election-specific deadlines.
  6. Use the correct federal passport form and finish employment, insurance and financial changes.
