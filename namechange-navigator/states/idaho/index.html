Idaho marriage name change: step-by-step agency guide

This guide addresses a surname change supported by a certified marriage certificate. If the intended change is not established by your marriage document, contact the relevant agency or a court about additional evidence. Government agencies set their own rules and may change fees or documentation standards.

1. Obtain an Idaho-certified marriage certificate

If the marriage occurred in Idaho, request a certified marriage certificate from the Idaho Department of Health and Welfare's Bureau of Vital Records. Certified marriage and divorce records must be ordered online or by mail; there is no public counter. Eligibility and identification checks apply. For online orders, use the Bureau's linked official ordering partner; a mail request is usually the more economical option. A copy accepted by Idaho DMV must show the official seal and recording document number. If the marriage was recorded in another state, obtain the certified record from that jurisdiction. Do not submit an ordinary photocopy in place of the certified proof.

Idaho Department of Health and Welfare — marriage-record ordering · Idaho Transportation Department — accepted name-change documents

2. Change your name with Social Security first

Use the Social Security Administration's official name-change service to request an update to your Social Security record and replacement card. Some applicants can begin online; others must provide evidence at an office. Idaho DMV requires the Social Security record to show your new name before it issues an Idaho driver's license or ID in that name. Its electronic verification compares information with SSA. Allow processing time and confirm that your updated record has been accepted rather than assuming a fixed waiting period.

Social Security Administration — name-change instructions · Idaho DMV — SSA name-matching requirement

3. Update your Idaho driver license or state ID at your county licensing office

Idaho instructs customers changing the name printed on a driver's license or ID to apply for a duplicate credential, or make the change with a renewal if eligible. Bring your current card, evidence of the SSA update and the original or certified recorded marriage certificate (or other acceptable connecting document) to the county driver's-license office, typically administered by the sheriff. Confirm local appointment rules before visiting. The current listed duplicate driver's-license fee is $20; ID replacement pricing and other transactions may differ. Review STAR Card / REAL ID identity and residency documents if you are upgrading or renewing. The DMV's 30-day address-change rule is for address changes; do not treat it as a verified name-change deadline.

Idaho Transportation Department — driver license transactions and current fees · ITD — documentary requirements · ITD — STAR Card identity checklist

4. Review vehicle title and registration in your county, if applicable

If a vehicle is titled or registered in your old name, consult the county assessor's motor vehicle office about the procedure for reflecting your new legal name. Ask whether a corrected title, registration record, lender approval or both are needed for your specific vehicle. Prepare your updated identification, existing title or registration and certified marriage certificate for review. Idaho's general vehicle-title page describes county title offices and transaction documents, but does not establish a universal marriage-name correction fee or filing deadline here; verify those details with your county before applying.

Idaho Transportation Department — vehicle titles and county assessor offices

5. Correct Idaho voter registration

VoteIdaho directs voters who changed their name to update their voter registration. You may update online if you have the Idaho identification accepted by the portal, or use a signed paper registration form delivered to your county clerk. The online and mail registration/update deadline is 11 days before an election; Idaho also provides eligible in-person registration options during early voting and on Election Day. Check your voter record again after you update it, and confirm the current election-specific details.

VoteIdaho — update registration after a name change · VoteIdaho — registration and deadline FAQ

6. Update your U.S. passport

Use the U.S. Department of State's name-change service to determine which passport application applies. Depending on when your passport was issued and your legal name changed, you may use Form DS-5504, DS-82, or DS-11, with different supporting-document and fee requirements. Match airline bookings to the passport you plan to travel with, and account for application processing and delivery time.

U.S. State Department — change or correct a passport

7. Update remaining records

Update your employer and payroll records, taxes, insurance, banks, credit accounts, medical records, schools, professional credentials and travel profiles with each organization. Keep evidence of what has been updated and retain certified marriage records as necessary. Idaho preparation checklist

  1. Order a certified recorded marriage certificate with the seal and recording number required for Idaho DMV.
  2. Update your Social Security name and confirm SSA has processed it.
  3. Bring your current credential and certified documents to the county driver's-license office; confirm duplicate-license versus renewal eligibility and transaction fee.
  4. Ask the county assessor's motor vehicle office about title or registration changes, if applicable.
  5. Update your Idaho voter registration and verify the record ahead of election deadlines.
  6. Check the applicable passport form, then update payroll, bank and other personal records.
