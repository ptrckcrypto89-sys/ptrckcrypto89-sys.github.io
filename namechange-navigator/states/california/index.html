California name-change steps

1. Request the correct certified marriage certificate

The California Department of Public Health - Vital Records (CDPH-VR) issues certified copies of public-marriage certificates for indexed record years. Its 2026 fee is $19 per copy. CDPH recommends requesting marriage records from the county recorder during the first six months after an event. Some years are not available through the statewide service.

For an identity/name change, obtain an authorized certified copy. Informational copies cannot establish identity. A mail request for an authorized CDPH copy requires a notarized sworn statement. If the marriage was confidential, obtain the record from the county clerk where the license was issued, not CDPH.

2. Update your Social Security record

Change the legal name with SSA before applying to California DMV. DMV verifies the name against SSA and may reject an application that does not match. SSA determines whether your case qualifies for online processing or requires an office visit.

Social Security Administration — Change name ↗

3. Complete a new driver-license/ID application and visit DMV

Start a new DL/ID application online, gather accepted legal-name evidence (and documents connecting earlier name changes when needed), then go to a California DMV field office. The name correction cannot be completed entirely online. A REAL ID application also requires proof of identity, Social Security number, and two California residency documents. Bring the existing credential and be prepared for the applicable DMV fee and identity checks.

Published standard Class C information-change fee: $37. A regular ID name change is listed at $40, with a reduced-fee ID at $11 and eligible senior-citizen IDs at no fee. Commercial and other transactions may differ. The DMV says a new driver's license commonly arrives by mail in three to four weeks.

4. Correct the vehicle registration/title if you own a vehicle

To change the owner name on a California title, DMV requires Statement of Facts REG 256, Section F. With the title available, update the name on it and submit the signed statement. If the title is missing, use REG 227 with ownership proof and applicable duplicate-title fee. If a lienholder holds the title, provide the REG 256 to the lienholder for submission with the title.

DMV's transaction manual says no fee is due solely for a legal name change on the registered-owner record, but renewal or replacement-title fees may be charged for separate transactions. The 10-day DMV notice associated with changes of ownership or address is not a general verified deadline for marriage-related name changes.

5. Re-register to vote in your new legal name

California requires a voter to re-register after a legal name change. The ordinary deadline is 15 days before Election Day. In most elections, conditional registration is available after the regular deadline through Election Day at designated county locations. The Secretary of State recommends first updating DMV, because old DMV signatures can conflict with the new voter record.

6. Update passport, employer and other accounts

Check State Department passport-name correction rules for the correct form, fee and timing; then update employment, payroll, banks, insurance, travel accounts and other name-linked records as applicable.

California checklist before you apply

  1. Confirm whether your marriage record is public or confidential and where to request it.
  2. Obtain an authorized certified copy suitable for identity purposes.
  3. Complete the SSA legal name update.
  4. Prepare original legal-name documents and evidence linking older names if needed.
  5. Complete the DMV application and visit a field office with applicable REAL ID proof.
  6. Verify the fee for your specific Class C license, ID or commercial credential.
  7. Update vehicle title/registration using REG 256 and, when needed, REG 227.
  8. Re-register to vote and confirm the relevant election deadline.
  9. Complete passport, payroll, financial institution and insurance changes.
