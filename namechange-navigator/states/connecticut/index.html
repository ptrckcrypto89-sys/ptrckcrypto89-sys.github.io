Connecticut marriage name-change process

1. Request a certified marriage record from the town or state

Once the marriage license is returned and recorded, Connecticut treats it as a marriage certificate. Request a certified copy from the town where the marriage occurred, a town where one spouse lived at that time, or the Connecticut State Vital Records Office. Connecticut DPH lists $20 per certified marriage certificate for both town and state orders; online VitalChek service may add fees. State requests can take longer than town requests.

Connecticut DPH — Marriage certificate instructions · DPH — Certified record fees and ordering

2. Submit the Social Security name change first

Request your corrected name record through SSA using the required legal-name and identity evidence. The Connecticut DMV specifically says your SSA update must be processed before the DMV appointment; SSA processing may take up to 48 hours. Do not assume the replacement physical card must arrive before taking another action unless DMV instructs it.

Social Security Administration — Change your name

3. Book an in-person Connecticut DMV appointment

Complete Change of Name Request Form E-78 and schedule a DMV hub or express office appointment. Bring your current driver's license/non-driver ID and original or certified legal-name evidence such as the recorded marriage certificate. DMV does not accept ordinary photocopies or religious marriage certificates as a substitute. Your prior physical credential is surrendered for a temporary paper card, and the corrected credential is issued through DMV.

DMV publishes a $30 name-change fee. Selected express/partner offices may charge up to $8 extra. DMV distinguishes this from renewing your license or changing your address; do not assume other transaction fees apply to your situation.

CT DMV — Name change, E-78, appointments · CT DMV — Current fee schedule

4. Correct your vehicle registration with Form B-301 if needed

For an existing Connecticut vehicle registration, bring your certified legal-name proof, current registration, and Registration Affidavit Form B-301 to the DMV appointment. The DMV specifically says the underlying certificate of title ownership does not change merely because the legal owner changed names after marriage. A license and vehicle registration can be updated at the same DMV visit.

Unable to surrender your current registration? The state's registration guidance lists a $20 duplicate-registration fee. This is conditional, not an automatic charge for every marriage name correction.

CT DMV — Update name on vehicle registration/title

5. Check or update voter registration

Connecticut permits eligible voters to update registration information through its online voter registration system, or by submitting a signed paper form to the local registrar. The Secretary of the State publishes a general 18-day pre-election cutoff and information on Election Day same-day registration for qualified voters. Confirm the relevant election and your new name in the voter record.

Connecticut Secretary of the State — Voter registration · Vote.gov — Connecticut registration and updates

6. Passport and financial records

The U.S. State Department uses different passport name-change forms depending on when you obtained the passport and legally changed names. Check DS-5504, DS-82 and DS-11 eligibility rather than assuming one form works in every case. Update employer payroll, banking, insurance, professional credentials and reservations using each provider's requirements.

U.S. Department of State — Passport changes

Connecticut preparation checklist

  1. Locate the town that recorded the marriage; request a government-certified certificate.
  2. Verify the certified copy matches your legal-name evidence; budget $20 plus any ordering fees.
  3. Submit a corrected name with Social Security and confirm processing before DMV.
  4. Complete Connecticut DMV Form E-78 and arrange an in-person office appointment.
  5. Bring your current license or ID, certified marriage record, required identity documents and $30 fee.
  6. If needed, prepare vehicle registration and Form B-301 for a same-visit correction.
  7. Verify your Connecticut voter-registration name and election deadline.
  8. Review passport form eligibility and update name-linked financial and employment records.
