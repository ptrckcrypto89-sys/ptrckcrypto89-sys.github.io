Arizona name-change procedure

1. Obtain the certified marriage record

Arizona marriage records come from the Clerk of Superior Court in the county where the marriage license was issued, rather than the state birth/death vital records program. Obtain a certified copy matching the evidence your agency requires.

Arizona State Library: marriage record custodians ↗ · Superior court fee schedule ↗

2. Update Social Security

Use the Social Security Administration name-change process, then wait two business days before visiting MVD as instructed by Arizona ADOT.

SSA name change ↗

3. Visit MVD with the legal documents

Within 10 days after your legal name change takes effect, visit an MVD or Authorized Third Party location. Original or issuing-agency-certified documents must show the prior and new name. ADOT lists a marriage license, qualifying domestic partnership or naturalization evidence, divorce or adoption decree, or court order among accepted proof. Your driver and vehicle registration records will be updated, and you will need a replacement license or ID.

Arizona ADOT: change your name with MVD ↗ · Driver license fees ↗

4. Handle vehicle title separately if required

ADOT says a marriage-related title update requires a signed application for title, a letter of permission and the applicable fees. The separate $4 lost-title replacement fee is not a verified marriage-title correction fee. Confirm with MVD, especially if title ownership or lienholder details are changing.

ADOT title update after marriage ↗

5. Update voter registration

Arizona Secretary of State allows legal name corrections to an existing voter registration online through AZMVDNow for eligible residents, by mail, or through the county recorder. Check upcoming election deadlines; standard registration deadlines fall 29 days before Election Day.

Arizona Secretary of State voter information ↗

6. Finish passport, employment, banking and insurance

The State Department handles passport correction separately. Complete payroll and other account updates using the institution's accepted proof requirements.

Passport name-change requirements ↗

Arizona checklist

  1. Order the appropriate certified marriage record from the county clerk.
  2. Update SSA and allow two business days for Arizona MVD record verification.
  3. Prepare original or issuing-agency-certified legal name-change documents.
  4. Visit Arizona MVD within 10 days and correct the driver record / credential.
  5. Confirm any title change requirements and applicable fees.
  6. Update voter registration before the relevant election cutoff.
  7. Review passport, employer and other name-linked accounts.
