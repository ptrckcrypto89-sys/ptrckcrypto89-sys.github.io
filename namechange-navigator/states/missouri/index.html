Missouri marriage name change: state and federal process

Missouri marriage certificates and marriage statements are different. The county Recorder of Deeds issues a certified copy of your actual marriage certificate/license, used for a married-name change. The state Department of Health and Senior Services issues a marriage statement, not the full certified marriage certificate. Also, a court petition is a different process from a married-name correction.

1. Obtain the certified marriage certificate from the Recorder of Deeds

Contact the Recorder of Deeds of the county where the marriage license was issued for a certified marriage certificate. The Missouri DOR expressly accepts the certified county marriage license with the issuing authority's stamp or raised seal, rather than an unofficial photocopy. Counties set their own ordering methods and certified-copy charges. If you do not know the county, the Missouri Department of Health and Senior Services may issue a state Certified Statement Relating to Marriage for $15, but this is not the original certified marriage certificate; the state has reports from July 1, 1948 onward. For a record created outside Missouri, request an official certificate from that jurisdiction.

2. Update the Social Security name record

Apply through the Social Security Administration with issuing-agency-certified marriage evidence and any required identity, citizenship, or immigration documents. SSA name corrections are free. Some eligible people can start online; others use the required form and local SSA service. Missouri DOR lists Social Security number verification among the identity-document categories for license issuance. Updating the SSA record before visiting the license office helps avoid mismatched identity records; the DOR guidance does not establish a universal mandatory waiting period after SSA processing.

3. Correct a Missouri driver license or non-driver ID

Visit a Missouri license office in person with your current credential, the certified marriage certificate, and any required evidence of identity, Social Security number and Missouri residence. The DOR takes a new signature and may retake your picture. An existing REAL ID holder with multiple prior changes may need a chain of certified documents connecting the names. If the credential expires in 184 days or less, you may qualify for an early renewal; otherwise, request a duplicate with the same expiration date.

State fee table: A duplicate adult Class F driver license is $16.50 for a 3-year license or $25.50 for a 6-year license; a Class ND nondriver ID duplicate is listed at $24. Other license classes differ. DOR has not identified a separate marriage-name-change deadline on its current instructions; do not assume that a 30-day rule from another state applies in Missouri.

4. Change Missouri vehicle title and registration

To reflect the married name on a vehicle registration, the Missouri DOR directs you to apply for a title in your new name. Bring the current original title, completed and signed Application for Missouri Title and License (Form 108), certified marriage certificate or court order, and fees to a local license office. This is a legal-name correction, not necessarily a transfer to a different owner. Missouri's official pages currently display different quoted title/processing totals for name-related transactions (the marriage guidance lists $14.50 for motor vehicles/trailers, while its title FAQ lists $8.50 plus $9 for a duplicate adding a name). Ask the servicing office for the precise transaction category and amount rather than assuming one of these totals applies to your specific title correction.

5. Update your voter registration and check deadlines

The Missouri Secretary of State permits name changes by online voter-registration application, mailed form or local election authority. Select Name Change and provide your former/current names as requested. The registration cutoff for a new election-eligible registration is generally the fourth Wednesday before Election Day (mail postmarked by that date); for November 3, 2026, that was October 7, 2026. For an already-registered voter who has only changed their name, the Secretary of State separately says to notify the election authority before voting or notify the election judge at the polling place; such a voter can vote one election under the former registered name after notifying the judge. Do not confuse an existing voter's name correction with first-time registration eligibility.

6. Review U.S. passport name-change eligibility

A U.S. passport under a former name is corrected using the form that matches your circumstances. Under State Department rules, DS-5504 may apply when both passport issuance and the legal name change occurred within the qualifying prior year; otherwise check DS-82 or DS-11. Submit a certified marriage document, current/previous passport, photo and fees according to the selected form. Confirm planned travel dates before mailing a passport.

7. Update payroll, finance, insurance, property and memberships

Once official identity records reflect the married name, update employer and payroll records, tax files, insurance, real estate/property interests, bank accounts, health records, professional credentials, and other service providers. Keep a certified marriage certificate available whenever an agency requires documentary evidence.

When Missouri requires a court order instead of marriage evidence

Missouri's RSMo § 527.270 authorizes a verified petition to the circuit court in the petitioner's county of residence for a name change that cannot be established from a certified marriage certificate. The judge must determine that the desired change is proper and not detrimental to another person's interests. Under § 527.290, newspaper notice is generally required after a decree, subject to statutory confidentiality exceptions for certain victims of domestic violence and related crimes. Court filing and publication charges are separate from government ID fees.

2026 statutory update: Effective August 28, 2026, Missouri § 527.270(2) prohibits a person required to register under §§ 589.400–589.425 from changing their name during the registry requirement. This legal restriction is distinct from routine name-record updates and should be assessed against current law when relevant.

Missouri preparation checklist

  1. Order a certified marriage certificate from the original county Recorder of Deeds, not just a state marriage statement.
  2. Update your name through SSA with original or certified evidence.
  3. Bring certified proof of name change and identity records to a Missouri license office; check duplicate versus early renewal fees.
  4. Complete Form 108 and submit the original vehicle title if your vehicle registration/title needs correction.
  5. Update voter registration, checking the four-Wednesday deadline versus the already-registered name-change exception.
  6. Choose the correct passport application and update financial, employer and insurance records.
