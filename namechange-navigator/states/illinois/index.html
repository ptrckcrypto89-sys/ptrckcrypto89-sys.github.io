Illinois marriage name change: step-by-step agency guide

This guide covers a marriage-related name change supported by an eligible certified marriage record. A different change that is not established by your marriage document may require a separate legal process. Confirm the latest agency requirements for your specific circumstances.

1. Request your certified marriage certificate from the county clerk

For an Illinois marriage, obtain the certified marriage certificate from the county clerk in the county where the marriage occurred. The Illinois Department of Public Health does not issue certified marriage certificates; it can provide only a $5 verification of certain marriage facts, which is not a substitute for a certified certificate when original or certified legal proof is required. County clerk ordering methods and fees vary. For a marriage outside Illinois, contact the jurisdiction that registered it.

Illinois Department of Public Health — marriage records and county clerks

2. Update your name with Social Security

Apply to correct your Social Security record and request a replacement card. Depending on your situation, SSA may allow you to start online or may require an office appointment. SSA accepts original documents or copies certified by the issuing agency, not standard photocopies. Correct your SSA name early to minimize inconsistencies when updating identification, employer and financial records.

SSA — update your name · SSA — acceptable proof of name change

3. Notify Illinois Secretary of State and obtain corrected license or ID

Illinois law requires notifying the Secretary of State within 10 days of a legal name change. For a corrected driver's license or state ID, Illinois requires an in-person DMV visit, surrender of the existing card and documentation connecting old and new names. Bring the original or certified marriage certificate and the identification requested on the state's corrected-license checklist. Additional certified linking documents may be necessary if your name changed more than once. The standard corrected driver's-license fee is $5, and a standard corrected state ID is $10 for ages 18–64; exceptions apply. Illinois's 2026 Rules of the Road booklet also describes a 30-day corrected-card window, while the current driver-services page emphasizes 10-day notification—report within 10 days and arrange the in-person correction without delay. CDL holders must obtain a corrected credential within 30 days.

Illinois Secretary of State — corrected driver license and 10-day notice · Illinois SOS — corrected license document checklist · Illinois SOS — current licensing fees

4. Correct Illinois vehicle title and registration, if applicable

If your name appears on an Illinois vehicle title or registration, the Illinois Secretary of State says you must correct the title before changing the registration. Complete Application for Vehicle Transaction(s), Form VSD 190, and submit the current Illinois title with marriage evidence. The state lists a $15 reduced corrected-title fee for a qualifying owner's name change due to marriage, plus a $3 corrected-registration ID card fee when applicable. The general corrected-title fee is higher, so clearly document the marriage-related basis for the reduction; confirm lienholder and mailing arrangements before submission.

Illinois SOS — corrected titles and marriage reduction · Illinois SOS — updating vehicle registration

5. Update Illinois voter registration

Update your existing voter registration to show the new name using the Illinois Online Voter Registration Application if the registration window is open, or through your local election authority. Online registration normally closes 16 days before an election; if it has closed for the upcoming election, check in-person grace-period registration or updates at your election authority's designated sites. Illinois's online application is currently closed for the November 3, 2026 general election, so do not assume an online submission will be accepted for that election. Check your voter record after the change.

Illinois State Board of Elections — online registration and current availability · Vote.gov — Illinois registration and update options

6. Update passport and other records

Use U.S. State Department guidance to determine whether DS-5504, DS-82 or DS-11 applies based on your current passport and when the marriage/name change happened. Separately update your employer/payroll, banks, insurance, travel profiles, medical records, tax records and professional licenses with each organization. Illinois preparation checklist

  1. Get a certified marriage certificate from the county clerk where your marriage was recorded; a state marriage verification is not equivalent.
  2. Correct your Social Security name record and assemble original or agency-certified evidence.
  3. Notify the Illinois Secretary of State within 10 days; visit the DMV to obtain a corrected license or ID with certified connecting documents.
  4. Correct the vehicle title before the vehicle registration when applicable; request the marriage-related reduced title fee.
  5. Update your Illinois voter registration or contact the election authority for grace-period options when online registration is closed.
  6. Determine the appropriate passport form and update employer, insurance and financial records.
