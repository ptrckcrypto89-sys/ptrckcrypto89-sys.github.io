Indiana marriage name change: official agency checklist

This guide addresses a name change supported by an official marriage record. If the name you want does not follow from your document, confirm whether you need a separate legal order. Fees and individual document requirements may vary by transaction.

1. Obtain a certified marriage license or certificate from the county clerk

If the marriage took place in Indiana, obtain an official certified copy from the Clerk of Court in the county that issued the marriage license. The Indiana courts' public lookup is useful for locating a record but is not a substitute for a certified copy. If the county is unknown, use the Indiana marriage-license public lookup to identify the issuing county, then contact that clerk. The Indiana Judicial Branch lists a fee of $4 for each certified marriage-license copy in its marriage-license instructions; confirm the clerk's current fees and delivery methods. If married outside Indiana, request the certified record from the jurisdiction that issued it.

Indiana Courts — marriage records and certified-copy offices · Indiana Courts — marriage-license public lookup · Indiana Courts — certified-copy fees

2. Correct your name with Social Security first

Apply for a Social Security name correction using the SSA's official online or in-person instructions. Have identity evidence and your original or certified marriage record available as directed. Indiana BMV verifies the legal name against Social Security records, so complete the SSA change before the BMV visit. Indiana specifically requires waiting at least one business day after the SSA change before arriving at a BMV branch.

SSA — change your name · Indiana BMV — SSA matching and wait period

3. Update your Indiana driver's license or state ID within 30 days

You must visit an Indiana BMV branch within 30 days after changing your name to obtain a corrected driver's license or identification card; the name-change transaction cannot be completed online. Bring the existing credential and original or issuing-agency-certified legal name evidence, such as an accepted marriage license. If earlier name changes create a gap between your documents, bring the connecting records. BMV requires identity and date-of-birth evidence that matches SSA records, and documents must be in English or accompanied by an accurate, verifiable translation. Check with BMV about REAL ID/residency evidence and your particular amendment fee before the visit; no universal name-change transaction fee is asserted here.

Indiana BMV — amend a driver's license or ID · Indiana BMV — in-person amendments and REAL ID requirements

4. Amend vehicle registration, and check the title separately

If an Indiana vehicle is registered in your previous name, BMV permits amendment of a registration because of marriage, divorce or court order. You must present proof of the legal name change when the name has not already been corrected on your Indiana credential; prepare the current registration and certified marriage record as well. If the vehicle title is also in the prior name, ask BMV how to handle that title before requesting an amended title—a change to your name is different from adding or removing a vehicle owner. A lien or electronic title can affect the steps, forms and charges. Do not assume the same documents or costs apply to both records.

Indiana BMV — amend a vehicle registration · Indiana BMV — title amendment information

5. Update Indiana voter registration

Indiana's official voter-registration portal allows existing registrants with a qualifying Indiana driver's license or state ID to update their registered name online at IndianaVoters.com. Without a qualifying credential, obtain and submit the VRG-7 paper registration application through a county voter-registration office or other authorized channel. The statewide pre-election registration deadline is generally 29 days before the election; verify the current election calendar and registration status directly, especially close to Election Day.

IndianaVoters.com — official voter portal · Indiana.gov — updating a registered name · Indiana state voter information — deadlines and record checks

6. Update your passport and remaining records

The U.S. Department of State's process depends on when your passport was issued and when your legal name changed. The applicable application can be Form DS-5504, DS-82 or DS-11, with different documentation, fees and in-person requirements. Then separately update your employer/payroll, financial accounts, professional credentials, insurance, medical records and travel profiles. Indiana preparation checklist

  1. Get a certified marriage record from the issuing Indiana county clerk or the appropriate out-of-state jurisdiction.
  2. Complete the SSA name change and wait at least one business day before the BMV visit.
  3. Visit Indiana BMV in person within 30 days to amend your driver's license or state ID; bring original or certified proof.
  4. Update vehicle registration when applicable; confirm title-specific rules with BMV.
  5. Update your Indiana voter registration and check the relevant 29-day pre-election deadline.
  6. Confirm the correct passport form and update employer, insurance, banking and other private records.
