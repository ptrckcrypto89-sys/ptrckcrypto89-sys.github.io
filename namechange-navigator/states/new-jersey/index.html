New Jersey marriage name change: official agency sequence

New Jersey drivers must notify the MVC of a legal name change within two weeks. The published NJ Driver Manual states that rule under N.J.S.A. 39:3-9a. A proposed bill to extend this deadline is not evidence that the current law has changed. Certified marriage or civil-union documentation supports an appropriate married-name correction; when the desired change is not legally established by that record, use the separate New Jersey Superior Court name-change process.

1. Order a certified New Jersey marriage certificate

Request a certified marriage certificate from the municipality that recorded the marriage or the New Jersey Department of Health, Office of Vital Statistics and Registry. State copies cost $25 for the initial search and certified copy, with $2 for each additional copy of the same record ordered together; municipal office charges vary. The marriage-license application fee ($28) is not the fee for a certified recorded certificate. Identity agencies expect an original or properly certified record with an issuing authority seal, not a decorative ceremony document or ordinary scan. When married outside New Jersey, obtain the certificate from the issuing jurisdiction instead.

2. Change your name with Social Security

Request a no-fee Social Security name correction using an original or issuing-authority-certified marriage record and current identity/citizenship or lawful status evidence as required. Some applicants can start through SSA's online name-change process; other applicants need a visit or additional documents. Update your SSA record early to avoid mismatches later, but the MVC's official marriage-name change procedure is governed by the documents it specifies and you should not miss New Jersey's two-week MVC notification deadline while waiting for a new Social Security card.

3. Notify the NJ MVC within two weeks and obtain a corrected license/ID

The New Jersey MVC handles name changes in person, as a walk-in at any MVC Licensing Center without an appointment for the name correction. Bring your existing driver license or ID, original or certified marriage/civil-union certificate or another qualifying legal-name document, and documents meeting the 6 Points of ID requirements. The legal name must be linked to the name on your birth certificate or earlier identity documents. If any required document is not in English, use an approved translation. The current fee chart lists $11 for an auto driver's-license correction or duplicate, separate from normal renewal costs. The MVC now mails replacement credentials rather than printing them at the counter.

Statutory deadline: The NJ driver's manual says a motorist who changes their legal name through marriage, divorce or court order must report it to MVC within two weeks under N.J.S.A. 39:3-9a; this is not the 30-day deadline applicable in some other states. Keep evidence of your timely report.

4. Correct the New Jersey vehicle title and registration, if relevant

If an NJ vehicle title still contains your former legal name, the MVC offers a title correction at an MVC agency or by mail with the original title and proof that a correction is needed. For non-MVC-caused corrections, the posted title-correction fee is $60; the MVC waives this charge when an error was clearly caused by the agency itself. A legal surname change is ordinarily not an MVC clerical error. Have a certified marriage record ready; request the correct name on the registration and confirm document reissue requirements with the agency. Do not confuse changing the existing owner's name with selling a vehicle or adding another owner. Those are distinct transactions.

5. Update the NJ voter-registration name and follow election rules

New Jersey allows name changes to voter registration through the Secretary of State's online voter-registration application, a paper form by mail, or the county commissioner of registration. The general 21-day voter-registration deadline for November 3, 2026 was October 13, 2026. Crucially, the law distinguishes the already-registered voter who changed their name from a first-time unregistered applicant. Under N.J.S.A. 19:31-13, if an eligible already-registered person's name-change notice was not filed by the 21-day cutoff, they may vote under the old registered name at the first election after the change by signing the register using both the old registered name and the new name. Contact the county elections office about identification and any other record discrepancies. This does not waive new voter-registration deadlines.

6. Correct the U.S. passport when needed

The U.S. Department of State's name-change route depends on passport age, issuance date, timing of the name change and renewal eligibility. DS-5504 may apply if both the passport issue and legal name change occurred in the qualifying past year; otherwise check DS-82 or DS-11. Follow the selected process for certified marriage evidence, prior passport, photograph and applicable charges. Match travel reservations to the passport you intend to use.

7. Update employer, financial, insurance and other records

Update employer payroll and tax withholding, insurers, banks and creditors, vehicle insurance, professional registrations, real estate accounts, schools, healthcare and other records after government identity documents reflect the intended name. Keep the original certified marriage record secure and supply only the documents actually requested by each organization.

When a New Jersey court order is required

A marriage certificate typically establishes a married surname without a separate judge's order. For a change not supported by the marriage record, New Jersey provides an adult Superior Court legal-name-change petition. The New Jersey Judiciary offers official self-help instructions and forms. A petitioner generally files in the Superior Court's Law Division, Civil Part in the appropriate county and supplies documents the current packet requires. Service, notifications and hearing rules can depend on circumstances. Court filing and certified-order fees are different from MVC and vital-record copy charges. Check the current court packet rather than assuming the standard married-name process meets petition requirements.

New Jersey preparation checklist

  1. Obtain the certified marriage certificate from the issuing municipality or NJ vital records (state first certified copy $25).
  2. Request a free Social Security name correction with original or certified proof.
  3. Notify MVC within two weeks and visit a Licensing Center with certified evidence and 6 Points of ID documents; check the $11 correction cost.
  4. Correct your vehicle title/registration as needed, noting the $60 ordinary NJ title-correction fee.
  5. Update voter name records and check the existing-voter old/new signature rule for the November 2026 election.
  6. Choose the appropriate U.S. passport form, then update employer, bank and insurance records.
