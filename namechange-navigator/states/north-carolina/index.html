North Carolina married-name change: verified process

North Carolina requires a corrected driver's license or ID within 60 days after the legal name change. NCDMV checks the new identity record with Social Security, so update SSA at least 24–36 hours before the DMV visit. The government-issued certified marriage license or certificate can establish a married surname; court-ordered changes follow a separate procedure. A title name correction additionally requires the corrected N.C. driver's license before application.

1. Obtain the certified marriage certificate

For a North Carolina marriage, request a government-certified marriage certificate through NC Vital Records (which manages marriage records from 1962 onward) or the county Register of Deeds office where the marriage license was issued. For older marriage records, use the issuing county Register of Deeds. Prices, search fees, identity requirements and delivery times depend on the issuing office and selected order method; verify the exact current charges when ordering. Marriage evidence for NCDMV must be certified and issued by an appropriate government agency—not a wedding souvenir certificate. Out-of-state marriages require evidence from their respective issuing state/local authority.

2. Change your name with the Social Security Administration first

Use SSA's free legal name-change service, supplying the original or issuing-authority-certified marriage certificate and any required identity/citizenship or lawful-status evidence. Eligible applications may begin online; others need an appointment or record submission. The NCDMV name-change policy expressly uses Social Security's verification system and tells applicants to update SSA at least 24–36 hours before their driver-license name correction, allowing time for federal and DMV records to align. The corrected Social Security card itself costs nothing.

SSA correction · SSA evidence · NCDMV SSA-before-license rule

3. Change the North Carolina driver license or ID in person within 60 days

NCDMV requires notification and a corrected duplicate credential within 60 days. Visit a driver-license office in person (online replacements are not intended for legal-name updates). Bring certified marriage certificate, divorce decree or court/Register of Deeds document establishing the new legal name, current identification, and the notarized DL-101 form when applicable (obtained from a DMV office). The official NCDOT support page currently lists $16.75 for a license name-change replacement. A name-change duplicate retains the existing expiration date; this is not an ordinary renewal. Check your exact credential and statutory amount at the licensing office.

4. Correct the vehicle title and registration if your car is in your old name

The NCDMV says to file Corrected or Substitute Title Application Form MVR-5 with a local license plate agency or its Title & Plate Unit. You generally need your updated North Carolina driver license first, the existing title if available, certified name-change proof and appropriately notarized title/application signatures, plus the transaction fee. If the title is held by a lienholder, the DMV can contact the lienholder to recall that title. This is a same-owner surname correction rather than a sale or addition of another owner. Ask the title agency for the current corrected-title fee and any related local charges; do not substitute a first-time certificate-of-title fee for a correction.

2026 change: Starting October 1, 2026, NCDMV moved to all-electronic vehicle registration and stopped requiring printed cards or license-plate stickers for new/renewed registrations. Corrected records still need to be requested as appropriate even though a new paper registration card may not be supplied.

5. Correct voter registration during the October 2026 election period

The NC State Board of Elections allows voter legal-name changes using a new voter registration form or, if you are already registered in that county, an update at a one-stop early voting location. The ordinary civilian registration cutoff is 25 days before an election; for the November 3, 2026 general election this was October 9. But during NC's in-person early voting window (October 15–October 31, 2026, ending at 3 p.m.), eligible voters can use same-day registration and update their name before voting. Ordinary first-time voter registration is not available on Election Day, except for narrow statutory categories. Use the county early-voting site list and the correct proof of residence/identity.

6. Correct your passport using the federal form for your circumstances

If the passport shows the prior legal name, the State Department may allow Form DS-5504 within the qualifying passport-issuance and name-change one-year window; otherwise review DS-82 or DS-11 depending on renewal or first-time issuance eligibility. Bring or submit the previous passport, appropriate certified marriage record, new photograph and any application fee specified by the form. Consider travel dates when submitting the passport.

7. Update payroll, insurance, finance and professional records

Once SSA and DMV reflect your legal surname, correct employer tax/payroll files, bank accounts, insurance policies, vehicle coverage, home or real-estate records and professional licenses. Keep secure certified marriage evidence for future identity verifications.

When a North Carolina court order is required

A certified marriage certificate normally supports a routine married-surname change. If the requested name is not legally established by marriage (such as a wholly unrelated first-name change), the Clerk of Superior Court handles the adult name-change special proceeding under NC General Statutes Chapter 101. Official Form AOC-SP-604 is the adult name-change application. Residency, identity documentation, criminal-record checks and other statutory requirements may apply. Court filing, fingerprinting and certified order fees are separate from DMV/license fees. Check court instructions for the applicant's specific situation rather than assuming married-name paperwork grants all legal changes.

North Carolina preparation checklist

  1. Request a certified marriage certificate from NC Vital Records or the issuing county Register of Deeds.
  2. Submit free SSA correction at least 24–36 hours before the DMV name-change visit.
  3. Get the corrected N.C. driver license/ID within 60 days, using certified documents and notarized DL-101 as required.
  4. Correct vehicle title/registration with MVR-5 and the newly corrected driver license.
  5. Update voter registration or correct your legal name at one-stop early voting through October 31, 2026.
  6. Choose the proper passport form and align financial, payroll, insurance and other accounts.
