Wyoming marriage name change: state and federal steps

Official-source checklist: Only a legally supported marriage-name change normally proceeds without a separate court order. These are agency correction steps, not a substitute for filing government forms or receiving a licensed attorney's advice. Read the official references before filing; fees and local processes can change.

1. Obtain the certified marriage certificate

Order a certified Wyoming marriage certificate through Wyoming Department of Health Vital Records Services using its official online, mail or in-person procedures. Confirm document eligibility, government-issued identification, current fee and processing method before ordering. Where a marriage was performed outside Wyoming, request a certified record from the government authority that recorded it. An unrecorded ceremonial certificate is not a substitute for legal proof.

2. Correct your name with Social Security

Request a corrected Social Security card directly from SSA with original or issuing-authority-certified marriage evidence and required identity or lawful-status documentation. This legal-name update is FREE. Some applicants can begin online; others must visit or submit original documents. Your Social Security number stays the same. Agencies may need time to synchronize, and an SSA correction does not automatically correct your driver license, title, bank accounts or passport.

3. Update your driver license or ID

Wyoming DOT requires an IN-PERSON visit to any driver exam office for a legal married-name change. Correct SSA FIRST because WYDOT electronically verifies the new name and birth date; an unmatched record may require presenting the Social Security card in the new name. Bring the current Wyoming credential, original or certified marriage record and other name-chain documentation. The applicant surrenders the old card, signs the new name and gets a new photo. WYDOT charges the STANDARD RENEWAL FEE rather than a nominal name-change-only amount, and normally mails the renewed license (valid 5 years) in 4–6 weeks. Do not confuse a separate address-update rule with a marriage-name deadline.

4. Correct vehicle title and registration as appropriate

Coordinate an updated owner name on Wyoming vehicle title and registration with the COUNTY CLERK or county title office. Bring the original title if held, lender/electronic title details, updated driver's license and certified name-change evidence. A same-person corrected owner surname is not a sale to a new buyer or automatic addition of a spouse. Confirm whether a new title and associated fee are required for your situation.

5. Update voter registration

Wyoming voters who changed their legal names must RE-REGISTER or amend the voter registration with the county clerk. Wyoming is exempt from the DMV Motor Voter system, so revising a driver license does NOT automatically update voter registration. Normal advance registration cutoff is 14 days before the election, but the state permits eligible voters to register and vote together during in-person absentee voting or ON ELECTION DAY at the polling place with required identification. A voter generally must have lived in Wyoming at least 30 days before the election. Use the current November 3, 2026 election calendar and county clerk's location.

6. Update the U.S. passport when needed

The U.S. State Department chooses Form DS-5504, DS-82 or DS-11 based on when the passport was issued, the timing of the legal-name change and renewal eligibility. DS-5504 may be available for certain qualifying recent changes with no standard application fee; other cases require different forms and applicable payment. Provide a certified marriage record, prior passport and photo as required. Verify travel-booking names before submitting a passport.

7. Complete other legal-name updates

Align employment payroll and tax documents, insurance, bank and loan accounts, retirement beneficiaries, professional licensing, school and health records, property records and memberships. Keep the certified marriage record so you can establish a legally documented link between names when the updated photo ID is not enough.

When Wyoming requires a separate court name-change process

If the recorded marriage evidence does not legally substantiate the name you wish to use, consult Wyoming's district-court name-change process for the applicable petition, residency, notice and filing fee requirements. Do not treat a newly issued driver license as a court order.

A certified marriage record does not necessarily authorize an unrelated first- or middle-name change. District or circuit court petitions, identity checks, notice, sealed records and fees differ by state and circumstance.

Wyoming preparation checklist

  1. Request the issuing-authority-certified marriage record.
  2. Update the Social Security legal name and ensure records align.
  3. Visit or contact the designated driver's-license office with original/certified evidence, observing state deadlines.
  4. Correct the titled vehicle owner's name only where a change is appropriate.
  5. Update voter-registration records under the state's election rules.
  6. Check passport correction eligibility and update employment, banks and insurance records.
