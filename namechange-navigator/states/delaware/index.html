Delaware name change after marriage: agency checklist

1. Obtain an official certified marriage document

Order a certified marriage certificate from Delaware's Office of Vital Statistics if your marriage was recorded in Delaware. The DMV requires an original or government-certified marriage document showing the legal name change; clergy-signed certificates are not accepted as a substitute. For marriages recorded in another state, use the issuing jurisdiction.

Delaware Vital Statistics — Certified marriage certificate request · Delaware DMV — Identity and name evidence

2. Correct your Social Security name first

Apply for the corrected Social Security record using SSA's official instructions. Delaware DMV specifies waiting 48–72 hours after the SSA update before visiting to allow database matching. Confirm your SSA name has been processed; the replacement card's arrival is separate from the database update.

SSA — Change name after marriage

3. Appear in person at Delaware DMV within 30 days

Within 30 days of legally changing your name, visit a Delaware DMV office in person with your previous driver's license or ID, original or certified proof of the change, and any linked prior-name documents. The DMV lists a $10 name-change credential fee. This is distinct from the $20 duplicate-license fee. Check accessibility, operating hours and evidence requirements before the visit.

Delaware DMV — Name-change timing and SSA sequence · DMV fee schedule

4. Change a vehicle title and registration, if applicable

Delaware DMV asks customers to update vehicle title/registration after a legal-name change. Bring the current certificate of title, registration card, proof of Delaware liability insurance and updated license (or legal-name change evidence). The department lists $35 for a new title and registration card in a standard no-lien name-change transaction; when a lender holds the title, the MV35 lienholder process and a $55 title fee may apply. Confirm the exact transaction charge.

Delaware DMV — Vehicle-title legal-name corrections

5. Update voter registration

Delaware's Department of Elections provides online and paper options for a change of name or address. The agency FAQ explains that an existing voter may update their name or address when voting, but recommends updating beforehand to reduce delays. Registration and party-change deadlines differ; check the relevant upcoming election rather than treating the new-voter registration deadline as a name-correction cutoff.

Delaware Department of Elections — Update voter registration · Official voter registration FAQ

6. Update passport, employment and private records

Use the U.S. Department of State's passport name-correction instructions to determine which form applies based on your document's age and timing. Then separately update your employer, financial accounts, insurance, travel profiles and professional licenses according to each organization's requirements.

U.S. State Department — Passport name correction

Delaware preparation checklist

  1. Get the issuing government's certified marriage record.
  2. Correct your Social Security name record and allow 48–72 hours for verification.
  3. Visit Delaware DMV in person within 30 days with old credential and name evidence.
  4. Confirm the $10 name-change credential fee and any personal exceptions.
  5. Update title and registration if a Delaware vehicle is affected; coordinate lienholder records if required.
  6. Update Delaware voter registration, passport and financial/identity records separately.
