Alabama name-change steps

1. Obtain your certified marriage certificate

The Alabama Department of Public Health provides certified copies of filed Alabama marriage records. It lists a $15 search fee including one certified copy, with additional copies of the same record ordered together for $6 each. Copies can be requested through authorized channels, including county health departments and mail.

ADPH marriage certificates and ordering instructions ↗

2. Update your Social Security record

SSA accepts evidence such as a marriage record to support a legal name change. Use SSA's online screening tool to determine whether you can complete the request online or must visit an office. Original documents or issuing-agency-certified copies may be required.

SSA name-change application process ↗

3. Correct your Alabama driver's license or ID

Alabama Law Enforcement Agency says to visit a local probate or licensing office or an ALEA Driver License Office. Bring valid legal name-change evidence (such as a marriage certificate, divorce decree or court order) and proof that SSA has updated your name. ALEA charges the usual duplicate license fee, and its published DL-100 lists that fee as $31.25. The special mail-in option is limited to qualifying applicants; do not assume it is available for your case. Check whether an appointment is needed before visiting.

4. Correct an Alabama vehicle title where applicable

Alabama Department of Revenue memorandum 2017-01 explains that a corrected title application is needed when a title owner's legal name changes. Accepted supporting evidence includes the title, identity evidence and legal proof of the change. The state lists a $15 corrected-title fee. Ask the issuing official about your exact transaction, including a lienholder or an out-of-state title.

5. Update voter registration

The Alabama Secretary of State allows its voter registration application to update an existing voter's name or address. Use the official online portal or submit the paper form to the county Board of Registrars. Check the applicable election deadline rather than assuming another agency automatically corrects this record.

6. Update federal travel documents and accounts

For a U.S. passport, follow the Department of State's instructions for your document's issuance date and legal-change circumstances. Then update employer, bank, insurance and other relevant accounts.

Checklist before you visit

  1. Verify your marriage record is filed and obtain a certified copy if needed.
  2. Complete the appropriate Social Security record change.
  3. Confirm accepted originals and proof of updated SSA record for Alabama licensing.
  4. Verify current office access, duplicate credential fee and STAR ID requirements.
  5. Update the title if the name on an Alabama vehicle title has changed.
  6. Check voter registration and other records separately.
