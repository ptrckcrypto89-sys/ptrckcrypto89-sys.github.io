Pennsylvania marriage name change: official steps

Pennsylvania law requires PennDOT notification within 15 days of a name change. A certified county marriage certificate is the standard proof for taking a spouse's surname. PennDOT's DL-80 name-change update card is free when no new plastic driver's-license card is requested, and the MV-41A registration-only surname correction is also free when no corrected vehicle title is printed. Requesting replacement physical cards/titles can introduce additional charges.

1. Order the certified marriage record from the recording county

Request a certified Pennsylvania marriage certificate from the Register of Wills / Orphans' Court office that recorded the marriage license. PennDOT's REAL ID instructions explicitly require a certificate issued and certified by the county office and reject an officiant's commemorative certificate or an uncompleted marriage license. County offices set their own certified-copy fees and ordering requirements. If married outside Pennsylvania, get the original jurisdiction's certified government record. Confirm that your marriage record connects the prior and married names for the credential requested.

PennDOT certified marriage evidence — county office

2. Request the free SSA name correction

Apply to the Social Security Administration with an issuing-authority-certified marriage record and any required identity or immigration-status documents. SSA name corrections are free. For a Pennsylvania REAL ID replacement after marriage, PennDOT specifically instructs you to update SSA first, bring the certified legal-name-change document, surrender the current REAL ID and have a new photograph taken. Keep certified evidence connecting any previous names shown on birth, passport or immigration documentation.

3. Notify PennDOT and correct your Pennsylvania license within 15 days

Under Pennsylvania Vehicle Code § 1515, the Pennsylvania driver's manual instructs licensees to notify PennDOT within 15 days after a legal name change. Submit Form DL-80 for a standard non-commercial license or the appropriate DL-54B for photo ID, DL-80CD for CDL; if renewing within six months of expiration, a different renewal form may be appropriate. **All legal-name corrections require an in-person Driver License Center visit**, using an original/certified marriage certificate or other valid legal evidence. The standard DL-80 change/correction-only option costs $0: PennDOT sends an update card carried together with the existing license. If you need a new physical photo credential (including REAL ID), a separate replacement fee may apply; do not confuse this with the free update-card option.

4. Update vehicle title and registration with MV-41A

PennDOT uses Application for Correction or Change of Name Form MV-41A. For marriage/divorce/pending divorce where you only need the registration record/card corrected and do not request a new paper title, complete Side A and include proof of your updated Pennsylvania driver's license or photo ID. No title fee applies for Side A. If you need a new title printed, complete Side B and pay the current Pennsylvania $72 title issuance fee. PennDOT's vehicle-name-change webpage still displays an older $67 amount for Side B, but its current authoritative statewide Payments & Fees schedule says $72; verify the current charge when sending your application. The update card must be obtained first to support the vehicle-name correction. Do not treat this as transferring ownership to a spouse.

5. Update voter name by Pennsylvania's 2026 cutoff

Submit a voter-registration Change of Name application through Pennsylvania's online voter registration, by mail or through your county elections office. The Department of State says existing voter-record changes must be submitted more than 15 days before the election to take effect for that election; changes received 15 days or less beforehand generally take effect after that election. For the November 3, 2026 General Election, the deadline was Monday, October 19, 2026. If you missed it, check your current registration and contact the county board of elections for instructions on the name shown on the pollbook, identification and voting. Do not assume a late name-change application updates the November 3 pollbook immediately.

6. Correct passport if applicable

Use the State Department's current form rules: qualifying recent passport and legal-name changes may use DS-5504 while other applicants use DS-82 or DS-11. Submit the certified marriage record, passport, photograph and applicable fees, following the selected route. Check travel reservations before surrendering documents.

7. Update other accounts

After federal and state records reflect the new name, update employer/payroll, taxes, financial institutions, property records, insurers, vehicle coverage, healthcare and licensing records. Keep the certified marriage record to show the link between previous and new names.

When Pennsylvania requires court action

For a desired legal name not established by the county-certified marriage record, adult applicants generally follow Pennsylvania's 54 Pa.C.S. Chapter 7 judicial name-change process in the Court of Common Pleas of their county of residence. The court process has filing, background-check, notice and hearing requirements, with confidentiality/waiver options in some cases. Court fees vary by county and are separate from DL-80/MV-41A changes. Do not assume a proposed procedural rule is already in effect; confirm the current county prothonotary's filing packet before proceeding. The married-surname correction ordinarily does not require such a petition.

Pennsylvania preparation checklist

  1. Obtain the marriage record certified by the county Register of Wills / Orphans' Court.
  2. Update SSA at no fee and confirm name matching for REAL ID or other identity records.
  3. Report name change to PennDOT within 15 days; submit DL-80 at a Driver License Center for a free update card if you do not need new plastic.
  4. Complete MV-41A Side A (free registration-only correction) or Side B ($72 new paper title, where needed).
  5. Update voter registration; the 2026 cutoff for November 3 was October 19.
  6. Correct passport and employer, bank, insurance and payroll records.
