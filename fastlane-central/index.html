Automotive technology

Diagnostic software built around the full vehicle picture.

Fast Lane Ventures develops custom automotive software that can bring trouble codes, live data, symptoms, and service history into one diagnostic workflow. Projects can help teams organize evidence, prioritize likely fault paths, monitor vehicle health, and plan maintenance—from shop tools to connected multi-vehicle systems.

Diagnostic decision support is scoped to the available vehicle data and validated project requirements.

Discuss an automotive project
OBD-II, ECU & CAN dataBring in trouble codes, freeze-frame, RPM and load, fuel pressure and trims, ignition and misfire, airflow, temperature, voltage, and throttle signals.
Multi-signal fault analysisCorrelate codes, symptoms, and live operating data to prioritize probable causes and the next diagnostic checks.
Guided troubleshooting & reportsCapture symptoms, inspection results, test steps, evidence, and clear reports for technicians and customers.
Predictive vehicle healthUse available signal trends and service history to flag emerging maintenance needs and health changes.
Repair shop & fleet platformsBuild vehicle intake, work tracking, service records, fleet dashboards, and connected maintenance alerts.
Vehicle owner appsOrganize vehicles, repairs, receipts, photos, modifications, maintenance reminders, and shareable service histories.
